## Adding a result

Before starting, copy [TEMPLATE.md](experiments/TEMPLATE.md) to
`docs/experiments/YYYY-MM-DD_short-name.md`. State the hypothesis, baseline,
changed variable, evaluation criteria, and stop rule. After the run, fill in
actual counts/outcomes and durable evidence paths, add one row here, and update
[STATUS.md](STATUS.md) only if the decision changes. Use explicit outcome labels
such as `success`, `partial`, `failed`, `no_score`, or `infrastructure_failure`.

Keep proposed work in the next-step list or an experiment's plan. A command
being generated, a synthetic audit passing, and a model finishing a scored trial
are separate outcomes.

The approved continuation was [high-resolution perception, supervised
closed-loop control, then gated model-free RL](experiments/2026-09-20-perception-supervised-rl.md).
SEER adaptation, predictive world-model control, reward learning, and imagined

near-port lateral error). Exact projected plug-tip/opening labels have passed a

complete: the oracle-keypoint ceiling passed at 0.124/0.235 mm median/p95, but
the observation-only locator failed at 2.380/6.947 mm and 82.6% direction
accuracy. Inference p95 was 3.311 ms. The policy and RL branches were stopped by

per-observation triangulation on 20 train and six new development starts.

near-port lateral error was 1.085/1.740 mm with 91.5% direction accuracy and

but remains promotion-ineligible. The policy and RL gates stay closed.

fresh six-start evaluation after calibration reached 0.289/0.531 mm. Held-out
near-port lateral error was the best so far at 0.278/0.670 mm with 94.1%
direction accuracy and 5.949 ms p95 inference, but it still failed the unchanged
gate. No policy or RL training followed.
A post hoc world-space filter reduced the diagnostic lateral result to
0.250/0.616 mm and 97.8% direction accuracy, but worsened axial error and did
not pass or qualify for promotion.
Heatmap-confidence camera rejection was also rejected during calibration in
favor of all three views, leaving the diagnostic result at 0.276/0.671 mm.

to 0.307/0.573 mm with 99.4% direction accuracy. A six-step causal variant was
