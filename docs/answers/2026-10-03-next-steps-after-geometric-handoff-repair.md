# Next steps after the geometric perception improvement

The new SC board-edge method measures orientation very accurately **on selected saved images**, and the SFP cross-camera rule fixes the two observed false candidate accepts **in an offline comparison**. The next question is whether the robot can use these results safely during a complete run. I would proceed in this order:

1. **Give perception an explicit “I cannot tell” output.** At a proposed handoff, the SC board-line method should return a valid angle only when it finds enough consistent lines in at least two cameras; otherwise it must reject the handoff rather than quietly use its rough learned angle. The SFP verifier must similarly reject weak identity or inconsistent camera geometry. Review examples where the requested opening is partly covered, offscreen, or confused with its neighbor. The existing projected-in-frame labels do not establish that the opening is actually identifiable.

2. **Run a small live handoff test without insertion.** Start transport from normal initial states. When its arrival mechanism proposes a pause, hold the measured TCP and run the selected-port verifier on fresh camera images. Record whether it accepts the **requested** port, rejects a wrong/hidden port, or resumes transport after rejection. The SC stop is still experimental, and SFP does not yet have a validated observation-only stop, so arrival itself is part of this test. Use up to **four new SC and four new SFP development resets** as an initial screening test, covering both requested ports and harder card layouts. Stop at the first false accepted handoff and diagnose it. Also record true plug/port pose **afterward for scoring**, route and contact/force, and complete ROS-to-decision p50/p95/p99; p95 must be below **300 ms**. Keep reserved final scenes sealed. Eight starts are a screening test, not enough to claim reliable performance.

3. **Repair whichever part fails, with a specific cause.** If transport never gets near the right port, work on transport and its arrival proposal. If it arrives but perception cannot identify a visible requested opening, improve visibility or identity evidence. If it accepts the wrong opening, strengthen the request check and test coherent wrong-port predictions; three-camera geometry alone cannot distinguish two consistently misidentified ports. If SC rails are hidden or misleading, change the viewpoint or reject until a better view is available. Reuse the same diagnostic starts to compare the targeted repair, then check new development starts; avoid generic retraining without a failure hypothesis.

4. **Attach and test the insertion actor only after the handoff works.** First replay the actual actor-generated waiting poses, because the earlier local BC actor sometimes retreated or moved laterally away even when perception was accurate. Train a bounded correction to that specific behavior using observation-derived port pose, not true simulator geometry at runtime. Then run complete autonomous Gazebo episodes from normal starts and report full insertion depth, target identity, lateral and orientation error, force, partial insertion, and latency by SC/SFP and requested port. The opening's roughly **0.5 mm lateral corridor** matters here; a good angle alone cannot guarantee insertion.

5. **Add recovery and RL after supervised full insertion is repeatable.** Collect real failures such as misalignment, force blockage, or cable snag from the complete policy. Keep the planned measured-motion backtracking as a safety/recovery mechanism and test whether a warm-started RL actor improves what happens after a backoff. Compare against the same policy without recovery and with local backoff. Do not begin RL merely because the perception component passed.

The immediate next experiment is **Step 1 plus the bounded Step 2 shadow handoff**. Success means no accepted wrong/unsafe handoff, reasonable retention of clear valid arrivals, safe resume after rejection, and complete live p95 below 300 ms. A failure identifies the next targeted repair; it does not justify moving to insertion or RL.
