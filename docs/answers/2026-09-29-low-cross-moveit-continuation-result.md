# Lowered SC MoveIt route: continuation result

I changed the route so the plug moves **away from the cards, left, and down together**, then travels beside the row while lower than the card tops. The highest NIC collision shape is 1.331 m high. Scored Gazebo measurements show the plug tip about 24–35 mm below that height during the traverse in three development layouts: five cards to SC port 0, five cards to SC port 1, and a shifted three-card port-0 layout. Each actual MoveIt joint trajectory reached its planned pre-insertion standoff with no Cartesian fallback. I inspected the synchronized overhead/side views: the cable can touch cards, but the arm and plug continue; no sustained snag or loss of progress is visible in these scored route-only runs. A brief contact or cable tension cannot be ruled out because there is no named cable/card contact stream.

With a **privileged** online insertion teacher after the route, one port-0 run fully inserted (official Tier 3 = 75, total 87.07), while an exact repeat stopped on near-opening contact. Thus this is **1/2** full insertions under that matched condition, not a reliable teacher or an autonomous policy result. On port 1, changing the wrist orientation only after passing the cards improved the handoff from an abort roughly 122 mm from the opening to a stop 3.9 mm from it. Its measured plug lateral error was still 0.87 mm as force rose, outside the 0.5 mm target corridor. Recovery and guarded-servo diagnostics did not fix it; the latter two stopped at the preinsert alignment gate. No BC or RL training was started, and final scenes stayed sealed.
