## Ordered plan

1. **Finish an honest image review before another fit.** Obtain a second, independent review of the 40 saved original-RGB panels, with the requested code but without projections or episode identity. Record the selected card and its two openings, or *uncertain*, separately for the current triplet and distance-spaced history. Reveal simulator geometry only after annotations are saved. The first review has 11 formally blind panels; the remaining 29 do not yet have a formal blind score. This will tell us which cases genuinely lack visual evidence and whether history helps. Budget: saved images only, no GPU or new rollout. Stop claiming the existing views suffice if sparse rows or neighbor swaps remain ambiguous.

2. **Test one view with a visible board reference.** First check geometry and collisions offline for a small wrist reorientation at the 180 mm standoff that keeps the full row and one fixed board end in at least two cameras. If feasible, run at most two reused nonfinal Gazebo pilots using the existing oracle contact, force and measured-return guards. Compare unmarked before/after images on the *same* reset. Stop this branch if the requested numbered card and port side still cannot be identified in two cameras, or if the view cannot safely return within 5 mm. The prior +80 mm and −80 mm lateral translations do not justify another broad translation sweep.

3. **Only if visual identity becomes observable, fit one association model.** Keep the precise single-point crop locator. Add a small full-row/context head that chooses which candidate belongs to the requested numbered card, with neighboring cards as hard negatives and an abstain output. Use complete reset splits and new nonfinal grouped scenes. The gate is zero accepted wrong cards, early usable pose in at least 9/10 groups, accepted 3D p95 ≤20 mm, and complete inference p95 <300 ms. Do not select a checkpoint on reserved final scenes.

4. **Repair transport with a matched, target-specific test.** The previous actor changed its first action by only 0.554 mm median when swapping the requested code on the same image, while teacher routes branched by much more. Once the visual identity gate passes, feed the identified card/board token and public request to one spatial transport candidate; compare with the current actor using the same replay and episode split. Stop before live rollout unless it improves early target-dependent action error and does not pick the wrong slot. Then require at least 2/3 safe buffered arrivals on new nonfinal starts, no extra contact, and p95 inference <300 ms. The existing spatial-actor plan explicitly forbids fitting before selected-target identity passes; do not bypass that gate by training on the three easy sparse starts.

5. **Integrate, then consider insertion and RL.** Enable transport → precise refiner → insertion actor only after both the identity and safe-arrival gates pass. Test autonomous insertion on new grouped development starts, including SC-to-SC and SFP-to-NIC as appropriate. Use RL for recovery from contact or cable blockage only after a supervised autonomous policy reliably reaches the relevant local states and the reward/force signals are validated. Keep the four reserved final configurations sealed until the full pipeline passes development criteria.
