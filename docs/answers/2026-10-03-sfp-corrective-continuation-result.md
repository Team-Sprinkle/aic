# SFP corrective transport continuation: result

I finished the bounded corrective-training experiment. A privileged CheatCode teacher could steer the arm back from the current actor's early deviation on **3/3 development starts**, without scored contact. I used ten balanced training recoveries, one for each SFP card/port request, to create **2,302 verified teacher action chunks after takeover**. The actor's own commands before takeover were excluded.

I then continued the existing full-action temporal transformer once. On held-out teacher observations, its SFP first-action p95 error improved from **3.13 to 2.47 mm**. In the actual autonomous Gazebo rollouts, it still reached the safe port-arrival buffer on **0/3 starts**, and **2/3** had scored contact. Its actor inference p95 was at most **103.17 ms**, so latency was not the failing gate. The two better routes reached 12.17 and 23.30 mm lateral error in the arrival band; the requirement is at most 10 mm. These are transport results, not insertion results.

The measured arm path separated from the matching teacher path by over 10 mm around **3.85–3.95 seconds** in all three starts. In the early 3–5 second interval, the actor's median first-command distances were roughly half or less of the teacher's. This supports a route-progress or next-waypoint problem, but the matched-clock command comparison does not prove a single cause once the paths diverge. More routine correction examples under the same action-only design are not justified by this result.

The first ten collection scenes passed causal label checks. An eleventh scene completed but was not extracted or trained on; nine further launches failed before simulation when rootless Docker reached the user's **200/200 kernel session-key quota**. I stopped four confirmed idle old containers and left the active Isaac GPU-0 job alone. Offline cache and training ran without sudo on local GPU 2; the three final development rollouts ran through rootless Docker on GPU 1. Reserved final scenes stayed sealed.

The next proposed experiment uses **existing teacher data** to predict the route stage and next waypoint explicitly. Budget: one GPU, at most **2,000 probe updates and two hours**. It proceeds only if complete-reset held-out stage accuracy reaches **90%** and waypoint translation p95 is at most **20 mm**. Then one actor continuation of at most **3,000 updates** may be tested on three newly frozen development starts. It must achieve at least **2/3 safe buffered arrivals**, no more contact than the frozen baseline, and complete inference p95 below **300 ms**. Rootless Docker's key quota must be repaired without interrupting the Isaac job before further live trials. PoseInsert, RL, automatic visual handoff and final scenes remain gated.
