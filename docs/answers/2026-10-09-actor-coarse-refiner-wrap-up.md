# Actor, coarse perception, and refiner: October 9 wrap-up

This is an audit of the saved experiments, not a new training run. “Worked” below means a clear improvement on a matched or episode-grouped development test; it does **not** mean the complete robot inserts autonomously. No model in this stack has passed the full-start, automatic-handoff, full-insertion gate. Reserved final scenes remain sealed.

## What the intended system does

1. A **transport actor** reads three wrist cameras, measured robot state and history, and the public task code (task family, requested card and port). It predicts four complete short TCP/body-frame commands. The task code must tell it *which* port to approach. It does not receive simulator port coordinates.
2. A **coarse selected-port stage** uses the same public request and camera views to decide whether there is enough evidence for a requested-port crop and a possible pause. It must reject a nearby *wrong* port. A pause proposal is only a request for closer inspection.
3. A **native-image refiner** crops the original camera images around the coarse prediction, estimates the requested opening more precisely, and combines camera views into a 3D pose. A separate checker must establish target identity, usable view, posture, force and a safe approach before handoff.
4. A separate **local insertion actor** would consume the visually predicted port pose and issue *complete port-frame target actions* near the opening. This actor has been trained experimentally, but it is not connected to an automatic full-start deployment. No autonomous insertion result is claimed.

The current experiments contain several checkpoints for each stage, rather than one promoted, end-to-end three-model release. Exact stage boundaries also differ across the SFP and SC shadow experiments. The [active handoff plan](../SELECTED_PORT_HANDOFF_PLAN.md) and [status](../STATUS.md) record which gates remain closed.

## 1. Transport actor and stop proposal

**Current approach.** A causal, three-camera temporal BC policy predicts full TCP/body-frame action chunks from RGB features, 32D measured state/history and a public 10D task code. The clean mixed-task training source contained verified SFP CheatCode routes and **83 SC Low Lateral Bypass training episodes** (21 SC development episodes). The SC source comprised 104 scored successes from 200 attempts; the separate cleaned derivative removes scripted quiet holds while preserving recorded action targets. Later diverse SFP collection added 136 successes, for **445 SFP training and 27 grouped development episodes** in that experiment. Thus the ordinary transport actor was **seriously trained**, not a two-episode pilot. However, the different checkpoints did not all consume the same newest data, and the actor that was run in recent SFP Gazebo diagnostics is an **experimental actor-prefix correction**, not a promoted all-data transport policy. [SC dataset and matched BC](../experiments/2026-10-01-sc-llb-cleaned-bc.md) · [diverse SFP source](../experiments/2026-10-06-sfp-diverse-transport-and-proposal.md) · [actor-prefix checkpoint and live gate](../experiments/2026-10-07-sfp-early-branch-support.md).

**Clear component improvements.** More balanced SFP demonstrations lowered new-scene teacher-action p95 from **1.856 to 1.668 mm**. A matched actor-prefix correction lowered held-out corrective-action p95 **2.897 to 2.338 mm**; in its three truly new Gazebo starts it avoided two contact penalties that the control incurred. A paired-request loss made same-observation requested-route differences more accurate (**7.71 to 3.65 mm p95**) and increased task-code sensitivity, but it also worsened ordinary SFP action error. These are useful diagnostics, **not a safe-arrival gain**. [Data-scale and proposal study](../experiments/2026-10-06-sfp-diverse-transport-and-proposal.md) · [paired-request study](../experiments/2026-10-07-actor-proposer-coarse-continuation.md) · [actor-prefix live comparison](../experiments/2026-10-07-sfp-early-branch-support.md).

**What did not work.** The matched actor-prefix candidates and controls both reached **0/3 safe SFP buffered arrivals** on truly new starts. An earlier corrective candidate reached **1/3 versus 0/3**, below its 2/3 gate, and chose a wrong-card route on a five-card start. Four SC BC variants gave **0/16 full insertions and 0/16 stable 10 mm handoffs**. Cleaning holds, longer history, route/stage labels, FiLM/request conditioning, spatial attention, and a from-scratch action-head reset each exposed some narrower benefit or failure mode, but none established reliable autonomous transport. Simply appending predicted port offset was ignored by the actor: paired action p95 was **2.95322 versus 2.95296 mm**, and zeroing the offset changed actions only **0.032 mm p95**. The October 8–9 MoveIt and side-route videos used privileged planning/teacher targets for diagnosis; **no new route actor was trained from them**. [SC controlled comparison](../experiments/2026-10-01-sc-llb-cleaned-bc.md) · [pose-use and training-method diagnosis](../experiments/2026-10-08-sfp-pose-actor-training-method.md) · [latest route gate](../experiments/2026-10-09-sfp-cable-side-route-repair.md).

**Stop probability.** The selected ordinary actor checkpoint has an untrained handoff slot (`handoff_aux_loss=0`). A *separate frozen-actor* pause head really was fitted with 445 SFP training episodes and was timely on **26/27 teacher development routes**, but falsely proposed a pause on **1/3 saved off-route autonomous runs** and remained high after the proper window. It is an **uncalibrated, shadow-only proposal score**, not a deployed probability of safe handoff. The separate nonlinear proposal experiment improved some saved-route behavior but still missed a window. [Pause-head result](../experiments/2026-10-06-sfp-diverse-transport-and-proposal.md) · [current model clarification](2026-10-05-observations-frames-and-current-models.md).

**Retraining decision.** Do **not** merely pool every old and new route and rerun the same BC objective. This actor already saw hundreds of demonstrations and several matched full-budget fits; new teacher-frame error gains did not transfer to autonomous arrival. The missing examples are safe, correctly directed actions at states **the actor actually reaches after drifting**, with a route/progress target that disambiguates the requested card. Only after the October 9 bounded stage-aware, cable-checked actor-state label gate passes should a new actor be trained on a curated, balanced union of the valid SFP and SC demonstrations **plus** those corrective states, with complete episode-group splits. Then rerun an independent autonomous buffer/contact gate. The SC 83-episode success source is real but thinner than SFP and has only 104/200 successful settings; it does not cover every difficult route or recovery state. Repeating the same fit for more epochs is not the identified repair. [Next label gate](../experiments/2026-10-09-sfp-cable-side-route-repair.md).

## 2. Coarse selected-port model and camera check

**What exists.** The first joint ImageNet-ResNet18 locator emitted candidate opening locations for **all 12 possible port channels** from reduced 288×256 camera images; the public task code then selected the requested channel. It was trained on **268 verified SFP and 104 SC episodes**, about **30,579 synchronized three-camera timesteps**, with 317/53 grouped train/development scenes. Its “in-frame” label came from projected simulator geometry: a port can be geometrically in frame yet hidden by a plug, cable or gripper. That label is **not true visibility** or calibrated confidence. The later direction is **one requested port**, with other ports treated as distractors, and the coarse stage owns crop proposal plus a *possible* pause; the precise refiner and checker must still approve it. [Original locator and labels](../experiments/2026-10-02-two-family-perception-local-bc.md) · [current handoff design](../SELECTED_PORT_HANDOFF_PLAN.md).

**Clear improvements.** The SC port-0/left-camera decoder repair eliminated >50-pixel wrong-object misses on two new resets (**6/15 and 15/16 to 0**), allowing native crops to contain the right opening. A requested-port **spatial** identity head, fitted with 83 ordinary two-port SC training groups plus **12 one-port fitting groups**, beat a matched pooled-feature head on four new one-port development groups: at the fixed all-three-camera rule it accepted **0/109 nonexistent-port requests versus 32/109** for pooled features, while retaining **96/100 clear projected-in-frame real requests versus 34/100**. This is a narrow counterfactual identity test, not certified visibility. Later frozen SC shadow checks approved the requested port on **4/4 new two-port starts** with 0.092–0.241 mm posthoc opening error and no scored contact. [SC spatial identity](../experiments/2026-10-02-sc-absent-port-identity.md) · [SC shadow result](../experiments/2026-10-08-handoff-only-shadow-and-memory.md).

For SFP, the strongest current observation-only **board prediction → requested native crop** pipeline was refitted on **91 train reset scenes / 5,406 three-camera groups**, selected on 27 separate scenes and scored once on 18 dense stress scenes. Early requested-port anchors improved **15/18 to 18/18** with zero accepted wrong ports in that stress set; accepted 3D p95 improved **3.17 to 2.22 mm**. Its measured preloaded-image board-plus-crop model-path p95 was **16.0 ms**; that excludes camera delivery and actor/controller time. This is a real grouped offline improvement, but the actor still rarely supplies a safe arrival. [SFP crop refit](../experiments/2026-10-07-actor-proposer-coarse-continuation.md#dense-view-native-crop-repair-result).

**What did not work or remains thin.** The original all-port locator confused neighboring/absent ports and accepted many geometrically in-frame but occluded SC views. Pooled SC presence heads and a last-layer patch missed absent requests; spatial evidence mattered. SFP requested-only low-resolution/history variants, simple coordinate-region stops, board screw/rail endpoint proxies and saved-port distance triggers all failed their identity, visibility or early-acquisition gates; a larger projected-endpoint training set alone did not fix ambiguous labels. The one-port SC spatial repair includes only **12 extra fitting reset groups** and four new one-port development groups; its clear improvement deserves preservation but does **not** establish all occlusions or reliable two-camera fallback. A one-port SC arrival later missed because usable board-orientation rails disappeared in the close view. The opt-in saved-orientation-memory repair showed **no causal live benefit** and remains off. No well-validated, true-visibility or calibrated-confidence head exists. [SC one-port limits](../experiments/2026-10-02-sc-absent-port-identity.md) · [failed SFP board data-scale study](../experiments/2026-10-07-sfp-rail-data-scale-and-visibility.md) · [handoff-only shadows](../experiments/2026-10-08-handoff-only-shadow-and-memory.md).

**Retraining decision.** Preserve the 91-scene SFP crop/board checkpoint and the SC spatial identity improvement. A blanket retrain on every available old image is **not indicated**: old reduced images lack native detail, and projected “in-frame” flags cannot become trustworthy visual-visibility labels by adding more of them. The SC one-port/occlusion checker **does need a more substantial, reviewed, episode-grouped dataset** before claiming robust visibility/identity; fit that targeted head and evaluate on new reset configurations, not on the four groups already used to develop it. Train a safer stop/proposal score with post-window and off-route **actor** negatives only if the transport actor starts arriving reliably. Avoid treating the successful teacher-route proposal score as deployed handoff.

## 3. Native-image pose refiner

**Current approach.** The coarse stage proposes a crop center from RGB alone. The refiner crops the original **1152×1024** image before resizing, predicts the *requested opening* within that crop and combines the three calibrated camera views. Simulator geometry supplies offline labels and posthoc error only; it does not select runtime crops. Port identity, pose accuracy and whether a view is safe to use are separate checks. [SC native-crop method](../experiments/2026-10-02-selected-port-handoff-execution.md#continuation-native-sc-crop-second-target-and-grouped-visibility-review) · [SFP crop refit](../experiments/2026-10-07-actor-proposer-coarse-continuation.md#dense-view-native-crop-repair-result).

**Clear improvements.** The SC native refiner was fitted on **83 SC training episodes**, selected on ten development episodes and checked on eleven further grouped development episodes. It reduced held-out pixel p95 **5.99 to 1.81 px** compared with coarse localization; near-port 3D lateral/axial p95 were **0.442/0.318 mm** in that test. Three additional new clear-view SC resets showed lateral p95 **0.230/0.175/0.149 mm**. These figures support precise *visible-opening position* in reviewed conditions, not complete 6D port pose or insertion. The older coarse checkpoint had used the broader SC development pool for selection, so the combined cascade is not fully independent end-to-end on the 11 groups. [Native SC fit and limits](../experiments/2026-10-02-two-family-perception-local-bc.md) · [new reset replay](../experiments/2026-10-02-selected-port-handoff-execution.md#continuation-native-sc-crop-second-target-and-grouped-visibility-review).

For SFP, the earlier native crop/correspondence pilot used only **29 successful episodes** (19 fitting, five selection, five further development). It reduced some keypoint/acceptance errors but retained a wrong-port acceptance, so it was **not sufficiently established** for deployment. The later **91-scene** requested-crop refit, described above, is a much stronger position/identity component result and supersedes the 19-scene fitting claim for that specific crop network. It does **not** automatically retrain or validate every older pair-correspondence, orientation or visibility checker. [Small SFP native pilot](../experiments/2026-10-03-native-crop-handoff-gate.md) · [larger requested-crop refit](../experiments/2026-10-07-actor-proposer-coarse-continuation.md#dense-view-native-crop-repair-result).

**What did not work or remains thin.** SC full orientation remains weaker than opening position. A one-port crop-orientation residual trained on only **1,524 examples from seven usable one-port fitting groups** improved matched held-out p95 **4.62 to 3.70°** but missed the **3°** requirement; its spatial variant was worse. It is a **small pilot**, not a solved one-port orientation model. The one-port board-rail view can disappear close to the port. SFP early 29-episode pair correspondence still had a wrong accept on its small further-development sample. Neither high position precision nor a fast component benchmark proves a safe automatic handoff, and no complete connected live p95 under 300 ms has been established for transport + coarse + refiner + local insertion. [Orientation and SFP correspondence test](../experiments/2026-10-03-native-crop-handoff-gate.md) · [one-port live miss](../experiments/2026-10-08-handoff-only-shadow-and-memory.md).

**Retraining decision.** There is **no evidence that the already strong SC clear-view position refiner needs a wholesale retrain**; it had 83 fitting episodes and independent clear-view checks. Keep its checkpoint and test hard one-port/occluded/viewpoint cases with proper reset separation. The 91-scene SFP position crop also does not need routine more-epoch retraining; preserve it and test on new actor-arrival views. In contrast, SFP pair identity and SC one-port orientation/visibility are genuinely **small-data or insufficiently validated submodels**. If those exact gates remain important after transport improves, gather targeted, visually checked original-image examples and retrain only the failing heads/components with a new independent reset test. Do not combine their pilot metrics into a claim that the complete pose/visibility estimate is solved.

## Overall decision

The highest-priority gap is **safe requested-port transport from actor-visited states**, followed by trustworthy automatic stop/identity under one-port, occlusion and wrong-request views. Precise clear-view port localization has shown substantial improvement and should be preserved. Rebuild or retrain the actor only after a safe corrective route-label source passes its predeclared cable/contact and held-out direction gates. The local insertion actor, trained most seriously on a **29–39-native-SFP-scene derivative** (despite a larger older low-resolution mixed-task source), is itself under-validated: one same-reset Gazebo comparison advanced 37.4 mm rather than 8.5 mm but **both scored zero**, and later small correction pilots did not establish insertion. It will need a broader, correctly framed native near-port dataset and matched Gazebo test **after** transport/handoff can deliver reliable starts. Keep automatic insertion and RL disabled until those gates pass. [Local actor result](../experiments/2026-10-02-two-family-perception-local-bc.md) · [latest status](../STATUS.md).
