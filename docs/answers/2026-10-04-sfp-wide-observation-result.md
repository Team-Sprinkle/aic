# SFP wider-view identity check — October 4, 2026

I completed the bounded wider-camera test on four reused, nonfinal SFP scenes. The robot briefly moved to a wider view, captured all three original-resolution cameras, then tried to return before the ordinary privileged teacher continued. Three routes returned within the 5 mm tolerance and the teacher scored 75. The fourth stopped at the unchanged pose tolerance; it did not attempt insertion.

The wider view made all four card faces visible in the first scene. In the five-card scenes, the cards were already visible before moving. Reviewing the **unmarked images before simulator labels**, I could see card faces and port openings but could not confidently match the requested *numbered slot* to one face in any of the four scenes. This matters most when there are empty slots: one three-card scene actually occupies slots 1, 2 and 4, so counting visible cards gives the wrong slot number. Posthoc geometry showed that the requested opening was already inside all three camera frames at all eight baseline and wide holds. Being in frame is therefore not the same as being correctly identified or truly visible.

The predeclared identity and safety gate failed. I did **not** train another perception model, enable the handoff, or run autonomous insertion/RL from these views. The next bounded test should use the saved images to identify the board's fixed slot reference and occupied slots, then check whether the requested opening is truly distinguishable; only a successful observation-only mapping warrants new episode-grouped training. The reserved final scenes remain sealed.
