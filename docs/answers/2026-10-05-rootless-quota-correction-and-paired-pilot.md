# Rootless Docker quota correction and paired-request pilot

Yes. You were right: the rootless Docker user-key quota was increased to **1024**. I misread `/proc/key-users`. The `115/115` value was the instantiated-key count, whereas the following quota field was below `1024`; `/proc/sys/kernel/keys/maxkeys` also reports `1024`. My preflight script used the wrong field and falsely blocked the experiment. I fixed it, checked the live value, and corrected the main status, experiment, handoff, output-map and machine-summary documents.

With the corrected check, I ran all six planned **training-only, same-board alternate-request** Gazebo CheatCode trials sequentially on GPU 1. The unrelated Isaac job on GPU 0 stayed active. **Five of six fully inserted and reached the measured buffered approach region** (axial −100 to −70 mm, lateral ≤10 mm), exceeding the planned 4/6 teacher-data gate. The sixth scored tier 3 = 25 and ended about 40 mm from the port; its nearest lateral distance while in the axial band was 13.46 mm. I preserved its scored run for diagnosis and excluded it from success-only BC targets.

I extracted the scored command/camera streams, converted the five successful alternates and their six original requests into TCP-body complete-action transport labels, and rebuilt a grouped cache with nonoverlapping episode IDs. The matched frozen-backbone task-gated fit trained on the 50 balanced episodes plus these 11 teacher episodes. On the unchanged ten development episodes, held-out first-target translation p95 improved from **3.236 to 2.937 mm (9.3%)**. That misses the predeclared **20%** offline gate, so I did **not** run the new actor autonomously or connect the insertion handoff. Its same-observation ten-request output diameter rose from 0.489 to 1.258 mm median, still a weak response compared with the early route branching seen in the teacher pairs.
