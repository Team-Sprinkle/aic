# Tilt-before-return result

I changed the five-card SC port-1 MoveIt route to **move behind and beside the cards, tilt at the clear beyond-row point, return aligned, then descend for insertion**. The return also rises 26.9 mm to the validated insertion standoff; it is not perfectly horizontal. During the distinct tilt segment, the measured TCP moved **1.59 mm** while the plug tip swept **8.78 mm**, confirming the pivot effect you identified.

The revised route reached the opening and completed full insertion in **2/2 exact repeats** of the latest privileged Gazebo teacher settings. One earlier setting also inserted. A matched older plan, with the same new insertion settings but orientation blended into its beyond-row sweep, inserted in **1/2** repeats. Those counts are encouraging, but too small and the plans differ in timing/holds, so they do not prove tilt timing alone caused the improvement. A longer-backoff setting was present in the two latest successful runs but **never activated**; it gets no credit for the successes. Other targeted trials stopped at tracking gates or near-opening contact. Brief cable/card touch was allowed; I found no sustained snag in the inspected route video.

I also advanced the next route-generality gate with one repeat each of the saved five-card port-0 and shifted three-card port-0 route-only plans. Both reached standoff with about **5 mm** measured TCP endpoint error and no scored off-limit contact or excessive force. They did not attempt insertion.
