# What the ±1 cm route tests showed

The original five-card route fully inserted in **5 of 7** fixed-scene runs (nominal plus six one-axis ±1 cm changes). Lowering the crossing behind the cards improved that to **6 of 7**. In both versions, shortening the forward move by 1 cm produced only about **10 mm partial insertion**. These were privileged Gazebo teacher routes on **one all-left five-card scene, SC port 0**, so neither result measures general reliability. The [low-cross results and recordings](/data1/chmin/yj/ws_aic/src/aic/docs/experiments/2026-09-26-sc-far-return-route-sensitivity.md) include the [short-forward failure video](/data1/chmin/yj/ws_aic/src/aic/outputs/experiments/2026-09-26_sc_far_return/low_cross_variations/forward_minus10/all_views_20fps.mp4).

We already made a limited **2 cm forward-margin change**. At a 145 mm nominal forward distance, the diagnostic controller inserted at 145 mm, but its 135 and 155 mm checks were partial. The corrective teacher inserted at 115 and 135 mm but was partial at 145 mm. An earlier 135 mm diagnostic had inserted, so the outcome is not a simple “move farther and it works” threshold. The partial runs still had roughly 12–15 mm sampled cable-center-to-card-box gaps and no scored force/contact penalty. Those measurements do **not** prove the cable was clear, but they also do not identify a cable snag as the reason for the partial insertion.

**A wider ±2 cm search might find another successful route; the current evidence does not suggest it will, by itself, find a robust one.** A robust route needs a *range* of nearby settings to work, not just one successful point. Here, nearby forward settings repeatedly changed the final insertion outcome, and the same planned 135 mm waypoints produced different outcomes on separate runs. That points to final approach, controller handoff, or simulation sensitivity as an unresolved factor.

I would next compare full and partial runs at the first moment their plug-tip alignment, orientation, cable shape, and measured TCP motion diverge. Then use MoveIt for the free-space path around the cards, validate the resulting cable path in Gazebo, and retest a small ±2 cm corridor-margin grid **with the same final insertion controller**. Continue only if full insertion and cable clearance hold across neighboring margins, both SC ports, and new starts. A single successful wider offset would remain a candidate, not evidence of robustness.
