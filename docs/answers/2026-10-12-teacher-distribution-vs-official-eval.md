# Teacher-route and released-evaluation check, October 12

I tested the current route-diffusion transport actor on the **same held-out scene configs** used by the two teacher generators: three SFP varied-start CheatCode scenes and three SC Low Lateral Bypass scenes. The original six teachers all inserted fully without scored contact. With the teacher removed at runtime, the actor inserted in **0/6**. It missed the approach buffer in all three SFP scenes. In SC, it took a different route, did not progress into insertion, and had no scored contacts in these three runs. These reviewed SC failures therefore do not establish a cable snag.

The divergence begins early. The SFP measured TCP path moves more than 20 mm away from the time-matched teacher by simulation time 4.2–4.45 s; SC does so by 6.05–6.30 s. On the exact held-out SFP teacher observations, the checkpoint predicts the recorded first targets to about 0.7–1.6 mm, yet its live actions are smaller or differently directed. This is a **teacher-state versus actor-visited-state gap**, with a train/live input difference also contributing.

I found that new SFP teacher images had been encoded at JPEG quality 86 before the shared image transform, whereas live SFP inference skipped that encoding step. **Correction after auditing the Docker launcher:** the first attempted ablation recorded the codec flag in the manifest but did not pass it to the policy container. Its changed offsets, **66.7/44.8/44.0 mm** versus **37.5/9.6/57.5 mm**, are an unchanged-policy repeatability result, not a codec benefit. The launcher now forwards the flag, and true matched reruns are being recorded separately. This codec change is **not promoted**.

I then checked the [released qualification configuration](https://github.com/intrinsic-dev/aic/blob/main/aic_engine/config/eval_config.yaml) against our local copy; their SHA-256 hashes match. The [official qualification description](https://github.com/intrinsic-dev/aic/blob/main/docs/qualification_phase.md) also cautions that future trial mixes may differ. On three separate, nonfinal **qualification-family SFP validation starts**, the unchanged-policy repeat reached **1/3** approach buffers versus **0/3** before. The offsets changed **68.9→72.8, 25.3→5.2, and 11.0→44.8 mm**. All still scored zero full insertion. The prior qualification-family SC check remains **0/3**; its rotated board lacks matching Low Lateral Bypass teacher coverage. These are bounded transport checks on scenes derived from the released config, not a claim of success on the exact full 180-second qualification suite or private final trials.

One measurement rule also needs repair: the old SC requirement that the TCP be within 10 mm laterally in an axial approach band rejects **10 of 21 fully successful held-out SC teachers**. The actor's SC failure here is clear from its route and lack of insertion, but that numeric rule should not be used as the universal SC transport gate.
