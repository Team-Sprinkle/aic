# SFP route-stage and waypoint experiment: result

I ran the agreed bounded probe on the existing **50 successful SFP training episodes and ten separate development episodes**. It used three frozen camera feature streams, measured robot state and public task code. The two labels were the current CheatCode route stage (approach or descent) and the measured TCP pose at the end of that stage, expressed in the current TCP body frame. True port geometry, teacher stage and future waypoint were **labels only**; previous teacher commands were zeroed to avoid an offline shortcut.

The 2,000-update probe passed stage recognition at **98.7%**, but failed its waypoint gate: **48.2 mm p95** error versus the required **20 mm**. A state/task-only baseline already had 98.6% stage accuracy and 50.2 mm waypoint p95, so the frozen global image history barely reduced the worst waypoint errors. The problem is concentrated in the initial approach (**67.6 mm p95**); descent reached **19.8 mm p95**. Even when the stage was correctly classified, approach waypoint p95 was 61.6 mm. The [held-out error plot](/home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-03_sfp_balanced_arrival_v1/balanced_bc/route_stage_waypoint_probe_v1/error_by_time_and_target_v2.png) shows this by time and requested card/port.

I checked for a simple label bug. Cached time exactly matched the source episode clock on checked episodes. At the approach-stage boundary, the measured TCP lagged the teacher's commanded endpoint by 13.0 mm median and 18.1 mm p95. That matters, but it cannot account for the roughly 48 mm overall p95. Initial original-resolution views in one high-error and one lower-error development scene show several similar visible cards and ports; two scenes are not enough to establish the full visibility cause. Coarse selected-target spatial grounding remains the leading hypothesis, not a proved diagnosis.

The predeclared offline gate failed, so **I did not train a route-conditioned actor or run a new live rollout**. PoseInsert, automatic handoff, RL and final scenes remain gated. The next proposed test is a task-conditioned coarse anchor for the **requested** card/port from original-resolution wide views, with simulator geometry used for labels only. Its first budget is one GPU and at most 2,000 updates. It should have zero accepted wrong-target predictions and ≤20 mm coarse position p95 on complete held-out resets before any actor continuation.
