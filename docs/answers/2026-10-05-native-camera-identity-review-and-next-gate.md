# Native camera identity review and next gate

I continued the camera-check data-quality gate. I reviewed four previously unseen request/image triplets in all three **original 1152×1024 cameras** using anonymous image aliases, and wrote the [judgments before opening simulator geometry](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v2/native_blind_aliases_20261005/blind_annotations_before_scoring.json>). I could see several SFP port faces, but could not confidently tell which visible face belonged to the **requested numbered rail slot** in any of the four. I marked those identities **uncertain**, not invisible or absent. [Posthoc labels](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v2/native_blind_aliases_20261005/scored_annotations.json>) show that two requested ports were physically present and two absent. This is a single-reviewer, four-case feasibility check, not the full 166-case review or a model accuracy result.

The twelve newly collected one-/two-card sheets were also inspected, but I had already seen their source episode names while auditing the dataset. I therefore did **not** count those images as blind evidence. The prior independent [ten-scene board-slot audit](../experiments/2026-10-04-sfp-board-slot-reference.md) recovered only **4/10** exact occupancy maps, with all five sparse layouts uncertain.

I also checked whether the proposed wider observation had already been tried. It **had**: the [four-scene wide-view experiment](../experiments/2026-10-04-sfp-wide-observation-gate.md) moved the wrist camera to a 180 mm standoff, then +40 mm up and +80 mm along the card row. More card faces were visible, but the strict raw-image review identified the requested numbered face in **0/4** cases, and one view route failed its measured-motion gate. The earlier ±15 mm side-view test also did not rescue the difficult card-2 request. Repeating these views would not be a distinct repair.

**Decision:** keep v3 as a valid synchronized **geometry/request candidate** dataset, but do not turn its projection flags into visibility labels and do not fit or deploy a camera-check/safe-pause head yet. Stop routine same-view data collection and the large annotation pass at this feasibility gate. Transport still fails its separate autonomous buffered-arrival gate, so insertion and RL remain disabled. Final scenes remain sealed.

**Next targeted experiment:** use existing native RGB and board CAD to test whether an observation-only model can detect the board's fixed rail origin and five occupied/empty slots, then choose the requested slot. The task code may select a slot but simulator board/port transforms must remain training/scoring labels only. Compare against the previous slot model on complete grouped development episodes. Cap the first fit at 2,000 updates on one GPU; require at least **8/10 exact occupancy maps**, at least two correctly mapped sparse scenes, zero confident assignment to an empty slot, and an improvement in requested-port early acquisition without accepted wrong ports. If that fails, stop fitting the same wrist views and design a genuinely different observation cue or pose. Even a successful offline diagnostic would still need new nonfinal autonomous starts and complete p95 inference below 300 ms before handoff.

The full dataset, scores, commands and this review are in the [experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-05-sfp-camera-check-data-quality.md).
