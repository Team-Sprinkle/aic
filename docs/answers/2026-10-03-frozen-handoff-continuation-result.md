# Frozen handoff continuation result

I completed the frozen transport-to-insertion handoff review and a bounded SC orientation repair. **The handoff gate still fails, so I did not enable the switch, attach the insertion actor, or start RL.**

- The SC selected-port identity check retained **5/5 valid stops** but also the **one known wrong-posture stop**. At that bad stop the tool was about 6.7° from the intended orientation. Knowing *which* port is visible is insufficient to decide whether the tool is pointed correctly.
- The SFP verifier rejected **4/4 saved wrong-card proposals** and retained **69/69 saved correct proposals**. Its three residual validation mistakes all confuse the two ports on **one card** in a single held-out reset; the openings are only about 22–24 pixels apart in the downsampled images. Those are point-level mistakes, not three proven full-camera handoff failures.
- I adapted the frozen-encoder SC orientation head using **196 three-camera examples from seven usable one-port training resets**, alongside the original SC/SFP training data. On **149 synchronized near-port observations from four new reset groups**, orientation error improved from **7.80° to 4.65° p95**. Original SC/SFP validation performance stayed near its prior level. A 3° check with the adapted head would reject the known bad frozen stop and retain **5/5 good stops**, but the independent 4.65° result misses the **3°** accuracy gate.

I checked a simple alternative using the direction between the two SC ports. Even with exact scored port centers, it gave **5.56° p95** on 21 held-out episodes, so that single direction does not determine the full mating orientation. I also fixed a per-camera timestamp/pose construction issue and rebuilt synchronized labels from existing streams; the corrected retrain gave essentially the same 4.65° result. No new Gazebo data was collected, and the final scenes stayed sealed.

The next targeted experiment is an **original-resolution crop of the requested port**, chosen from RGB rather than simulator coordinates, to improve SC orientation and distinguish adjacent SFP ports. I documented a budget of at most **12 targeted SC training resets, two development resets, four GPU-hours, and one day of image review**, with a required ≤3° independent SC p95, zero known unsafe accepts, ≥95% clear-stop retention, and complete live p95 latency <300 ms. The existing training resets must first be audited for board-pose coverage before any new collection.
