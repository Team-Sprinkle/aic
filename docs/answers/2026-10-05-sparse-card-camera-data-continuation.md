# Sparse-card camera-data continuation

I continued the coarse camera-check data audit and found a real training gap: the earlier 66 training resets had **no one- or two-card SFP scenes**. Those layouts appeared only in held-out actor diagnostics. I ran a train-only four-case CheatCode pilot in rootless Gazebo, using GPU 1 while leaving the existing Isaac job alone. Both one-card cases fully inserted, but both two-card cases redirected to the second card failed full insertion. The predeclared expansion gate was at least three successes in four cases, so I **stopped that collection at 2/4**. Two matched controls on the same two-card boards, with their original card-0 request, both fully inserted. The controls suggest a problem with the redirected teacher route; they do not show that all second-card insertions are easy.

I extracted scored controller and camera streams from the **four successful** episodes only. After applying the existing strict 10 ms synchronization rule, these contribute **2,026 three-camera moments**. The separate [v3 camera-check manifest](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v3/summary.json>) now has **3,690 training moments across 70 reset groups**. Pairing each image moment with all ten possible requested ports yields 36,900 training request-image rows. The new four episodes account for 20,260 of these rows, including absent-slot requests. These rows are correlated frames and counterfactual requests, not independent episodes. The development and diagnostic groups were not changed.

The [v3 audit](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v3/structural_audit.json>) passed the timestamp, request-expansion and split checks and decoded 120 sampled triplets / 360 original RGB files. A separate [scene-file audit](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v3/sparse_scene_independent_audit.json>) checked all 20,260 new request rows against saved card occupancy and task code: **zero mismatches**. I inspected original one-card and two-card images, including center/left/right views of a two-card approach. They show the expected board, plug and orange cable. The [one-card present-request image](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_sparse_camera_train_v1/rollouts/cards1_slot4_port1/frames/1791218741707084622_22750000000_center.jpg>) and [two-card center image](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_sparse_camera_controls_v1/rollouts/cards2_slot4_original_card0_port1/frames/1791219236364399831_17750000000_center.jpg>) are examples. The complete pilot scores, controls, commands and provenance are in the [experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-05-sfp-camera-check-data-quality.md#sparse-card-coverage-follow-up-october-5).

**What this does and does not establish:** the saved scene files correctly label which ports physically exist, and the camera/state frames are synchronized. That does **not** mean the requested numbered opening can be identified in every image. In a two-card approach, the other card can be outside the current view; near the port, the gripper can cover the opening. The earlier ten-scene blind slot review identified only 4/10 exact occupancy maps. Consequently I did **not** train a visibility, camera-check or pause classifier from the new geometry labels, and no automatic handoff was enabled.

**Next bounded gate:** finish a blind original-RGB review that deliberately includes sparse slots, absent requests, near-port occlusion and off-route actor views. The separate four-case original-resolution review found requested numbered-slot identity uncertain in all four cases. An earlier, already completed wider observation experiment had also failed its ≥3/4 identity gate (0/4 strict identifications, one measured-motion abort), so repeating that view is not the next step. Instead, test whether a detector can recover the fixed rail origin and occupied slots from existing original RGB. If identity and visibility labels prove reliable, then build separate safe-pause labels from causal force, orientation and route state and run one ≤2,000-update grouped-development camera-check comparison. Require zero accepted wrong ports, at least 9/10 early correct acquisitions and complete inference p95 below 300 ms before live handoff. The four reserved final scenes remain sealed.
