# SC cleaned dataset and BC continuation — October 1, 2026

The SC Low Lateral Bypass source remains intact: **104 officially successful teacher episodes** from 200 attempts. All 104 were decoded and split by complete scene/reset group into **83 training and 21 validation episodes**. The motion-safe cleaned derivative retains **69,681 transport chunks** and all **26,455 local chunks**; it removes 19,570 quiet scripted-hold anchors while preserving recorded action targets, measured TCP motion, and force changes. The independent [dataset audit](../../outputs/trajectory_datasets/sc_llb_cleaned_v3/materialized/audit_motion_safe_v2.json) passed.

Four supervised transport BC variants finished: clean ImageNet initialization, older mixed-route warm start, motion-safe hold cleanup, and causal elapsed time. Their held-out first-target errors were approximately **4.2–4.8 mm p95** on teacher frames. That offline fit did not yield autonomous transport. On the same two non-final Gazebo development layouts, each actor ran with both 20 and 60 mm per-component action caps: **0/16 full insertions and 0/16 stable handoffs within 10 mm**. The best final handoff distance was **75.4 mm**. The larger cap and elapsed-time input did not repair the route. Complete inference p95 was at most **182.6 ms** across these runs. Reviewed traces and videos show early wrong route choices and late drift; the reviewed examples do not show sustained cable snag. These observations do not establish that cable snags cannot occur elsewhere.

The original score/bag/trace artifacts, checkpoint hashes, plots, and selected five-view 20 fps videos are retained under `outputs/experiments/2026-10-01_sc_llb_cleaned_bc/`. See the [full experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-01-sc-llb-cleaned-bc.md#final-bounded-supervised-result-and-step-5-decision), [offline comparison](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-01_sc_llb_cleaned_bc/offline_comparison.json), and [matched cap comparison](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-01_sc_llb_cleaned_bc/cap_comparison.json).

**Decision:** Step 5 remains open. The local pose-conditioned actor, visibility switch, RL, and reserved final scenes remain gated. The next proposed test is one supervised route-progress/subgoal actor using causal image, robot-state, task-code, and recent-action history. First audit route-stage labels on the existing 83/21 split. Train one GPU for at most **6,000 updates or 2 hours**, stopping on a validation plateau. Require at least **90% held-out stage accuracy**, **20 mm or less p95 next-subgoal position error**, and no stage skip in replay before live testing. Then run at most two 70-second rollouts on the same development layouts. Continue only if both reach and hold the 10 mm handoff, follow the correct cable-side route with acceptable force, and keep inference p95 below 300 ms. Otherwise revise the transport architecture rather than collecting or retraining routinely.

The commands and exact data paths are in the [experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-01-sc-llb-cleaned-bc.md). No new training or final-scene evaluation was started while closing this result.
