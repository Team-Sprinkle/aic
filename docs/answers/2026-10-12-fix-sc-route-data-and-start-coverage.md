# Fixing the SC route data and initial-condition coverage

**SC teacher consistency is fixed in the active dataset.** The 22 new SC training prefixes collected with CheatCode were removed from the active training union. The SC training file is byte-for-byte identical to the 83 verified Low Lateral Bypass (LLB) training episodes; its SHA256 is `c2a984ce522d30ec84e116036fa87f838ecf86b51eb9b9b1deaf5ffd464da653`. The old mixed union/cache and checkpoint are marked retired, and the old retrain launcher refuses ordinary use. Raw CheatCode recordings and scores remain as diagnostics. No model was retrained from the corrected data.

**Actual startup variation was repaired and tested in Gazebo.** The collector now changes the first-trial robot xacro, not only the YAML home-joint values, and compares the six measured startup joints in the scored bag with their predicted offsets. A label from a varied-start scene is rejected if that audit is absent or fails. The new SFP collections completed 40 scenes with 40/40 valid first-trial start audits. The worst start-prediction discrepancy was 0.01102 rad; every start differed from the old fixed start by at least 0.01256 rad in one joint.

The first 20 scenes tested the two released SFP requests with board/rail perturbations: 20/20 full insertions and 20/20 safe causal label admissions, split into 16 train/four development. A second bounded 20-scene pilot covered all ten requested SFP card/port combinations, once on each released board orientation, with one card present per scene: 18 full insertions, two contact-free partials, 20/20 valid starts, and **20/20 safe transport-prefix admissions**. The partial insertion tails were excluded and explicitly marked transport-only. The predeclared gate required at least 16/20 admissions and all ten request codes; it passed. No reserved final scene was opened.

The current active dataset is `/home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-12_route_consistent_union_v4/`: **533 SFP and 83 LLB SC training episodes**, plus **43 SFP and 21 SC grouped development episodes**. Its tracked pointer is [ACTIVE_TRANSPORT_DATASET.json](../ACTIVE_TRANSPORT_DATASET.json). The [experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-12-route-consistent-data-and-starts.md) has commands, source locations and safety gates; the [machine gate result](../experiments/2026-10-12-sfp-grid-pilot-gate.json) lists each of the 20 all-request attempts.

**Remaining limit:** This fixes the accidental SC route mixture and verifies randomized SFP starts and broad SFP request coverage. It does not prove coverage of undisclosed final evaluation settings. New SFP samples across every request used one card, while older verified data supply the multi-card cases. The older SC LLB set spans one to five cards and both SC ports but lacks the released rotated-board SC orientation near −1.8 rad. A direct transform of the old LLB route failed MoveIt planning at a low waypoint, so no rotated-board SC replacement was admitted. That needs a scene-specific LLB route and Gazebo cable check before further SC training. No autonomous policy improvement follows from this data audit alone.
