# Public evaluation coverage retrain: result

I audited the released [qualification scenes](https://github.com/intrinsic-dev/aic/blob/main/aic_engine/config/eval_config.yaml) against our verified SFP-to-NIC and SC-to-SC teachers. The old data covered 1–5 card counts and both port choices but missed the released second SFP board side and the released SC board yaw. I collected 40 varied public-family Gazebo scenes plus eight with the exact published three-card SC layout. The final split stayed sealed.

**Data admitted:** 47/48 recorded runs yielded causally checked transport prefixes: 16 SFP train, 4 SFP development, 22 SC train, and 5 SC development. The excluded SC run never reached the measured safe near-port buffer. Only fully inserting SFP runs were admitted. SC partial/no-insertion runs contributed only their earlier, contact-free measured approach, cut before insertion. A duplicate episode-ID bug was caught by the cache, fixed, and the failed partial artifacts archived.

**Training:** I retrained the route-aware diffusion transport actor from the same initialization as an unchanged-data control, for 6,000 updates each. It has 14.29M trainable parameters and an 11.18M frozen three-camera ResNet18 encoder. The coverage actor allocated 25% of each task family's training draws to the new scenes. On five held-out new SC teacher episodes, near-port first-action median error improved from 4.8 mm to 1.0 mm.

**Autonomous result:** The fixed comparison used three SFP and three SC development scenes, each run with both actors. Both actors reached the required axial and ≤10 mm lateral safe buffer on **0/3 SFP and 0/3 SC** scenes. The new actor reached the axial band on all six, but its closest lateral offsets there were 16.7–26.2 mm for SFP and 29.4–68.4 mm for SC. It had no scored contacts; the control had two. Complete SC inference p95 was 301.7–351.5 ms, above the 300 ms limit. **The new checkpoint is not promoted.**

The [target-frame trace plot](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-11_route_aware_diffusion_transport_v1/official_anchor_retrain/trace_diagnosis.png) and original side-view frames show the live actor moving near the board but beside the selected opening. On clean teacher observations it predicts a target-directed lateral correction; during its own rollout it often advances axially while laterally misaligned. This identifies **compounding error on actor-visited states** as the next repair target. Another identical BC fit on clean demonstrations is unlikely to address it.

The next bounded experiment is a small on-policy corrective-label pilot on **new training resets**, with a collision/force and first-movement-direction gate before any fit. If that gate passes, compare a corrective actor against a matched no-correction actor on the same frozen development starts. Separately test whether fewer denoising steps bring SC inference below 300 ms without changing the route.
