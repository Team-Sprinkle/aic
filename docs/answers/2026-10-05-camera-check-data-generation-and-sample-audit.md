# Camera-check data generation and sample audit

I built a request-conditioned SFP camera-check candidate dataset from the existing original-resolution three-camera recordings. Each image triplet is paired with all ten card/port requests, including absent ports and neighboring-port alternatives. The corrected version contains **1,664 training triplets from 66 reset groups** and **16,640 training request-image pairs**. Development and harder diagnostic reset groups remain separate.

I checked data quality before model training. The source had **92 problematic triplets**: 90 had an image more than 10 ms from the measured state and two mixed camera simulation timestamps. I excluded them in the corrected dataset, then audited every retained triplet's timestamps and decoded a random 360 RGB images. The audit passed.

I also visually inspected unmarked sample images spanning near, far, absent and partial cases, then inspected projected target markers on four original-image crops. This caught a crucial label issue: a port can project inside all three cameras yet be **hidden by the gripper housing** in the actual picture. [See the projected-point crop audit](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v1/projected_port_crops_audit.jpg>) and the corrected dataset's [unmarked sample sheets, 1](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v2/blind_visual_sheet_1.jpg>) and [2](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v2/blind_visual_sheet_2.jpg>). This is why the manifest explicitly leaves visual identifiability and safe-pause labels empty instead of treating projected geometry or distance as those labels.

I then checked 18 more near-port triplets from 18 different reset episodes. After looking at unmarked views first, the projected-point crops showed an obvious foreground obstruction at the selected point in **10/18 triplets**, across **14/54 camera points**. This is a conservative single-reviewer spot check of the *point*, not a certified label that the whole opening is invisible. The [counts and per-sample notes](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-05_sfp_camera_check_dataset_v2/near_port_projected_point_spotcheck.json>) are preserved.

I did **not** train a new camera-check head from incorrect visibility targets. The next bounded gate is to review the 166 stratified original-image triplets for actual requested-port identifiability, then build physically checked pause labels before fitting one matched model. The autonomous identity target remains zero wrong accepted ports and at least 9/10 early correct acquisitions on new nonfinal starts; final scenes remain sealed. Full counts, commands, source hashes and decisions are in [the experiment record](../experiments/2026-10-05-sfp-camera-check-data-quality.md).
