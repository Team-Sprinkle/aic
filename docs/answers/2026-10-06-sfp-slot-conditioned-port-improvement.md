# October 6: SFP perception continuation

I updated the main project, status, dataset, handoff, experiment and artifact-map documents with what we tried and what remains open. I also ran a bounded next repair for the SFP requested-port perception problem. **The model improved, but the automatic handoff gate still failed**, so it is not connected to the transport or insertion policy.

The previous slot-local model can now identify which of the five board slots contain cards on the inspected scene groups. It does not yet say precisely which opening belongs to the requested card and port. A two-camera 3D board-reference check removed wrong accepted candidates but yielded an early correct port in only **2/10** scene groups. A stricter horizontal-board fit yielded **1/10**. I stopped adjusting those geometry checks on the same scenes.

I then used the board model's **RGB-predicted rail positions** and the requested card code to place a **480 px crop in each original camera image**. The projected requested opening lay inside at least two crops early in **10/10** development groups and **10/10** additional nonfinal groups. This coverage check used simulator projections only to score the crops afterward; true geometry did not place them. Neighboring openings often shared the crop, so the detector still had to identify the requested one.

I trained a request-conditioned crop model for its full **2,000-update** budget on **21,262 present card/port requests** from **3,690 frames and 70 separate training reset groups**. Ten other groups selected the checkpoint. On ten additional nonfinal groups, the old checkpoint accepted 38 frames with one wrong-port acceptance and **4.46 mm** accepted 3D p95 error. The new checkpoint accepted **62 frames**, with **zero wrong-port acceptances** and **1.80 mm** accepted 3D p95 error. Both provided an early accepted pose in only **6/10** groups, below the predeclared **9/10** gate. The low 3D error applies only to accepted frames; it does not describe all frames or autonomous insertion.

In the four early failures, the target projection was usually inside the crop and all three camera heads were often confident. Their chosen points failed the three-camera reprojection check. An [original-image failure panel](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_slot_crop_failure_views_v1/card1_port0.jpg>) shows a camera choosing a neighboring opening. Green markers are model outputs; red markers are posthoc simulator projections. This points to **cross-camera target correspondence** as the next problem, more than crop size or an ordinary confidence threshold.

## Next experiment

Keep the frozen crop selector and detector backbone, retain several candidate heatmap peaks per camera, and select the combination that agrees across three cameras and with the requested port. Develop the rule using training and development groups only. Then evaluate on **new complete nonfinal reset groups**, since the additional ten groups above have already been inspected. Budget: at most one GPU, **2,000 learning updates or two hours** if training is required, followed by a bounded read-only evaluation. Continue only if it has **zero accepted wrong ports**, an early correct pose in **at least 9/10** new groups, accepted 3D p95 **≤20 mm**, and full inference p95 **<300 ms**. If that fails, collect visually reviewed selected-port and occlusion labels from targeted autonomous approach views; do not add routine teacher routes or tune on the already-scored ten groups.

Transport still reached **0/3 safe buffered arrivals** in its latest autonomous diagnostic. That is an independent prerequisite before switching to the refiner and insertion actor. The final scenes remain sealed; automatic handoff, PoseInsert insertion, and RL remain disabled.
