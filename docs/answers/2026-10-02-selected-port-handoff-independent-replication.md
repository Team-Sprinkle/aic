# Selected-port handoff continuation (October 2, 2026)

I continued the approved transport → selected-port perception → insertion plan. The main result is a **real but limited SC perception repair**. A tiny update to the frozen coarse locator now finds the requested SC port in reviewed clear views where the old model sometimes pointed at the gripper. It has not yet made pose estimation accurate enough for insertion or solved visibility and switching between actors.

## What changed and what was measured

- I found that an earlier apparent holdout used the **same complete scene configuration** as one fitting scene. I excluded it from generalization evidence and added a scene-hash guard. A corrected training rerun, using only the two fitting scenes, reproduced the saved final-convolution weights **bit for bit**.
- I tested the saved repair on **two genuinely different SC port-0 development resets**, one shifted layout and one shifted five-card layout. The requested opening was checked in the original RGB before the evaluation windows were fixed. In the left camera, large wrong-object misses fell from **6/15 to 0/15** and **15/16 to 0/16**. [See the marked before/after images](/home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-02_selected_port_handoff/sc_port0_left_independent_before_after_gallery.jpg): red is the scored opening for audit, green is the old prediction, cyan is the repaired prediction.
- Across all three cameras, predicted port position improved from **42.76 to 2.86 mm p95** on the shifted reset and **139.07 to 3.78 mm p95** on the five-card reset. More directly, the error **sideways to the port** improved from **16.64 to 1.43 mm p95** and **29.74 to 1.26 mm p95**. These are correlated frames from only two resets, and the numeric success threshold was written after the results, so this is a diagnostic rather than a model-promotion gate. The required insertion corridor is **0.5 mm lateral**.
- I corrected the 100-image original-RGB visibility review batch. SC crops in v1 were centered on the model's own possibly wrong point; [v2](/home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-02_selected_port_handoff/selected_visibility_review_v2/review_manifest.jsonl) centers audit crops on an independent scored aperture projection. The main manifest still has no certified visibility labels. A [separate provisional review of clear cases](/home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-02_selected_port_handoff/selected_visibility_review_v2/manual_triage_provisional_v1_summary.json) found four high-score false-visible SC examples among 20 clear negatives; it leaves all 50 SFP examples and 11 SC examples uncertain. This is three SC scene groups and one reviewer, so I did not train a classifier from it.

The stock CheatCode runs used to obtain the two new SC test views only **partially inserted** (tier 3 about 39.67 and 40.42); they are not new successful expert episodes. No active job was interrupted, no RL or insertion model was trained, no reserved final scene was opened, and nothing was deployed.

## Current decision

The coarse SC port proposal is improved, but the full handoff is **not ready**. Transport still lacks a validated pause-and-inspect proposal plus safe rejection/resume. Perception still needs checked visibility, selected-port identity under occlusion, native-resolution precision, and orientation. The next bounded step is to establish a three-way label rule on original RGB—**identifiable, not identifiable, uncertain**—including hidden requested targets with a visible neighbor. Uncertain images must not be forced into yes/no labels. Then compare the selected-port crop refiner with the frozen baseline on complete held-out resets. Require no known wrong-port or hidden false accepts, lateral p95 at or below 0.5 mm, and complete live inference p95 below 300 ms before connecting the insertion actor. If the label source cannot agree with inspected images, stop that training branch and repair the labels first.
