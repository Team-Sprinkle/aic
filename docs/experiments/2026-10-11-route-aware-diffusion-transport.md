# Route choice diagnosis and route-aware diffusion transport

## Observed failure

In the saved nonfinal five-card SFP rollout requesting card 1/port 1, both deterministic actors depart more than 10 mm from the successful teacher route around 5.4–5.5 s and more than 35 mm near 7 s. Early first-command direction cosine against a nearby teacher state is still about 0.93–0.95, so a modest heading difference accumulates. After 7.1–7.35 s it falls near 0.4 and the actor turns toward card 0. Prior audits found correct task codes, fresh cameras and no sampled frame-conversion error. This supports a route-grounding and recovery-from-drift hypothesis; the exact internal cause remains unproven.

The old temporal_cache_v4 contains 235 training SFP episodes requesting card 0/port 1 and only 7–14 for several other requests. The newer temporal_cache_divergent_round2_v1 contains 23–40 episodes for most other slots and 243 for card 0/port 1, across 408 training and 27 development SFP scene groups with zero overlap. Its 53,541 rows also include SC. This is the frozen source for the new fit.

## Repair and architecture

The revised sampler gives equal mass to SFP and SC, to each of the ten SFP requested card/port codes, and to each episode within a request. SFP 4–12 s decisions receive twice the weight because that is where departure began. No train/development scene is moved.

The new trainer is [train_route_aware_diffusion_transport.py](../../scripts/train_route_aware_diffusion_transport.py). It supervises one-second measured TCP displacement in the current TCP frame from the same teacher episode, then conditions its diffusion action denoiser on the *predicted* displacement. Future motion is a training label only. The action remains four complete recorded TCP-body targets, never a residual on an old policy. A frozen three-view ImageNet ResNet-18 supplies the same observation features. The 3-layer 384D history Transformer and 6-layer 384D denoiser have 14.29M trainable parameters; adding the roughly 11.18M frozen backbone gives about 25.47M total. The previous diffusion head had about 3.48M trainable parameters. The new model uses EMA weights, a cosine noise schedule and 12 deterministic inference steps.

The first epsilon-prediction fit was preserved as a failed baseline: its nearly zero terminal signal-to-noise ratio amplified small denoising errors into 156–204 mm held-out first-command p95 at update 8,000, despite an early SFP route-goal p95 of 3.14 mm. The trainer was corrected to predict diffusion velocity, whose clean-action calculation has no division by the near-zero terminal signal coefficient. The corrected fit uses a separate output directory and a 12,000-update cap; it does not reuse failed weights.

This is a testable model/data repair, not a proven autonomous fix. Global pooling of frozen images may still hide spatial detail needed for card identity or cable routing.

## Preset gates and budget

- One project GPU (GPU 3); initial 8,000-update diagnostic, then at most 12,000 corrected updates or four hours, with validation plateau stopping. Keep old models, failures and final scenes untouched.
- Report first-target errors by task, early SFP 4–12 s one-second goal error, requested card/port effects and card count. Before live use require early SFP goal p95 at most 20 mm, SFP and SC first-target p95 at most 4 mm, and a same-image request-swap check showing the expected change toward the alternate teacher route. This offline screen is permissive, not a superiority claim.
- Benchmark complete camera/encoder/denoiser p95 below 300 ms before any new nonfinal autonomous start. If the offline gate passes, run at most three starts; require at least two safe requested-port buffered arrivals and no worse contact. If the gate fails, diagnose visual grounding, future-goal labels and diffusion fitting before choosing another direction. Handoff, insertion, RL and final scenes remain separate gates.

## Completed fits and controlled diagnosis

Both fits used one project GPU and kept the final scenes sealed. The 12,000-update velocity fit finished at its update cap, with the best checkpoint at update 12,000. It used 46,761 training and 6,780 development rows. A further 4,000-update fine tune started from that checkpoint, drew only from 108 same-observation **training** request pairs with valid future labels, and added losses on their one-second route-goal and first-action differences. Its best checkpoint was update 3,500. The 13 held-out separable pairs cover six development scenes; none entered the paired loss. Exact commands are `run_vpred.command.sh` and `run_pair_ft.command.sh` beside the artifacts.

The actor uses the predicted route goal: replacing it with zeros changed the first action by 1.60 mm median and raised held-out first-action p95 from 2.77 to 4.10 mm. The offline position screen therefore passed. These values are from teacher observations; they do not prove off-route recovery. The previous deterministic long-history actor had a lower teacher-frame first-action p95 (2.62 mm SFP / 2.27 mm SC), so the diffusion fit is not superior by imitation error alone.

An optional `AIC_ROUTE_DIFFUSION_CHECKPOINT` path was added to the ordinary observation-only temporal runtime. It preserves the three-view ImageNet input, measured state, public requested card/port code, and TCP-body complete action targets. It does not pass simulator geometry or the teacher route to the actor. A runtime normalization assertion and actual Gazebo execution checked the adapter. The rootless evaluation launcher records the diffusion checkpoint hash.

On the **same previously used nonfinal five-card card-1/port-1 scene**, the old baseline and old corrected temporal actors had closest requested-port lateral errors in the axial band of 55.79 and 68.89 mm. The diffusion actor improved that to 17.99 mm with Gazebo and actor sharing GPU 3, and 20.49 mm with Gazebo on GPU 4 and actor on GPU 3. **Neither visited the safe buffer or inserted; autonomous route repair failed its promotion gate.** The official scorer said “No contact detected” on both diffusion rollouts. The measured TCP paths are in `fivecard_route_comparison.png`; `fivecard_route_diagnosis.json` and `fivecard_split_route_diagnosis.json` preserve the state-matched command analysis. Early actor commands had a positive teacher-direction cosine, then position and orientation drift accumulated. The same-GPU run first exceeded 10 mm position gap at 6.15 s and exceeded 20 mm at 6.95 s. This supports closed-loop drift after an initially plausible route; it does not isolate one model error from control lag and unrepresented actor-visited states.

The local 38 ms benchmark missed a deployment cost. An early ground-truth diagnostic gave 225 ms median denoising and 374 ms p95 complete inference. A later GPU-ordinal audit found that the alleged split-GPU profile did not guarantee distinct physical GPUs, so its 285 ms full-rollout p95 is an observation, **not proof that GPU splitting fixed latency**. The main diagnostic slowdown was the actor's ROS TF listener receiving a flood from the toolkit's `ground_truth:=true` launch. Delaying listener startup until the privileged teacher takeover restored representative actor cadence: 27 proposals in six simulation seconds and complete inference p95 of about 120 ms in the corrected takeover run. The ordinary autonomous actor never enables this special ground-truth mode. For the corrected pilot, process monitoring confirmed Gazebo on physical GPU 3 and policy on GPU 4. Any promoted actor must still pass its own normal live p95 check. Commands and traces are in the artifact directory, including `run_delayed_tf_profile.command.sh` and `run_delayed_tf_teacher_validation.command.sh`.

One bounded **privileged diagnostic**, not a training example or an autonomous success claim, handed control to CheatCode after six seconds of the diffusion actor on that same development scene. It fully inserted (official tier 3: 75) from the off-route state at simulation time 10.658 s. The recorded TCP moved only 0.55 mm in the first 0.2 s and 5.98 mm by 1 s after takeover, so label timing must be joined to the actual causal command. Oracle contacts found at most about 0.04 N sampled cable–board point force and no scored contact; wrist force had an approximately 20 N standing load both before and after takeover. This establishes recoverability on **one reused development scene only**. Its data must not be folded into training. See `takeover_diag/` and `takeover_diag/scored_stream.json`.

## Decision and next bounded repair

Do **not** promote this checkpoint. The remaining hypothesis is missing actor-state correction data, especially after the first 5–20 mm route deviation. The next candidate should use new **training** reset groups: run a frozen diffusion actor for 5–7 s, then a privileged teacher; retain only causally joined post-takeover observations and commands that reach the requested buffer without scored or high sampled cable/card force. First collect at most ten deliberately varied train starts; proceed only if at least eight yield safe, timely corrective labels, with different card/port requests represented. Keep complete groups separate from new development starts, fine tune the same architecture under a matched update budget with and without those corrections, and rerun autonomous Gazebo with split GPUs. Promotion still requires at least two safe buffered arrivals in three nonfinal starts and complete inference p95 below 300 ms. If the pilot fails, stop collection and redesign the corrective teacher or observation representation. The final split, automatic handoff, insertion policy, and RL remain closed.

The targeted model used the same 14.29M-trainable-parameter architecture, initial paired checkpoint, seed, batch, 4,000-update cap and 3e-5 learning rate as the matched control, but assigned the new corrections **3% of SFP sampling mass**, down from the failed broad arm's 10%. The first reused development scene failed the autonomous stop rule. On the same five-card card1/port1 reset, the targeted model made 309 proposals, never entered the -100 to -70 mm axial / ≤10 mm lateral safe buffer, and came within 52.31 mm laterally there. The earlier broad arm over-commanded at 5.36/2.26 mm. Same-clock path differences are descriptive only, because action timing differs.

The apparent train/development mismatch is concrete. Nine of the ten targeted training resets came from the older `n130__card0_port1_resume` collection and one from `sfp_nominal_multicard`; the reused development scene is `n400__ec2_02_sfp_hard`. The training starts' requested-port-frame lateral coordinates had median (71.6, 24.9) mm; the development start was (67.4, 81.2) mm. Only one training start had lateral-y above 70 mm, and its lateral-x was 27.8 mm. Thus the model saw few starts jointly like the development case. This difference, plus the correction fit/live gap, **supports** a coverage/generalization hypothesis; it does not prove the source collection name itself caused failure.

**Decision:** No autonomous full insertion, no safe buffered arrival on the reused development case, no checkpoint promotion, and no more collection or fitting within this branch. The next distinct experiment should select disjoint five-card `n400`-style **training** resets by *joint* starting port-frame offset and route difficulty, including high lateral-y starts around the failed case, then collect early actor-to-teacher continuations only if the teacher succeeds without damaging contact. Reserve new complete-reset development scenes before fitting. A bounded gate could use 8–12 such train resets, require at least 8 safe causal-label episodes, and compare the same 3% corrective model to the frozen matched control on three new development starts. Continue only if at least two of three reach the safe buffer at ≤10 mm lateral with no pre-handoff damaging contact and full p95 <300 ms; otherwise change direction to an explicit route/standoff controller or an action-conditioned visual representation rather than repeating the same fine tune. This is a proposed plan revision, **not work already run**. Final scenes remain sealed and RL, handoff and insertion training remain gated.
