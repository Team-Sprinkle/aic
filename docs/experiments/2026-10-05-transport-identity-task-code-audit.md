# Transport and selected-port identity: autonomous replay and task-code audit

Date: 2026-10-05. Status: **upstream gates failed; no deployment change**. All scenes here are existing nonfinal development or train configurations. The four reserved final IK configurations remain sealed. The Isaac GPU 0 job was untouched.

## Question and stopping rule

Why does the SFP transport actor miss the buffered region, and does the current coarse crop machinery identify the requested port on the images the autonomous actor actually generates? Diagnose the first divergence on saved traces, run one bounded task-conditioning repair (at most 2,000 updates on 50 balanced training episodes), and stop that repair unless held-out first-target translation p95 falls by at least 20%, every requested card/port stays below 5 mm p95, and later live arrival passes. No generic retraining, automatic actor switch, insertion, or RL follows a failed gate.

## Saved autonomous transport evidence

The same-scene teacher and actor three-camera montages at 4, 8, and 12 s are in [`outputs/experiments/2026-10-05_transport_identity_audit`](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-05_transport_identity_audit/). At about 4 s the views are similar; the actor route visibly separates by 8–12 s. The earlier measured same-scene path comparison put the first >10 mm deviation at about 3.7–4.2 s. These are three reused SFP actor runs, not a new-scene success estimate. The score and measured path—not resemblance in a selected video frame—control the arrival decision. Prior [coordinate-proxy replay](2026-10-05-global-coordinate-arrival-proxy.md) found 0/3 actual safe arrivals and two misleading nominal-coordinate stop points, at 31 and 99 mm lateral error.

To test whether the actor reads the public request, the frozen ordinary temporal SFP actor received exactly the same held-out image, measured state, and causal history ten times. Only the requested SFP card/port bits changed. Across ten complete development episodes and five sampled ages each, the median diameter of all ten first-action translation predictions was **0.489 mm**; the median gap to the nearest alternate request was **0.147 mm**. Thus the output is almost invariant to the request in this diagnostic. The input code itself was checked: `[SFP, SC, port0, port1, card0…card4, valid]`. The original checkpoint has heavily uneven underlying successful SFP episodes (card 0/port 1: 232; most other combinations: 5–7), despite target-balanced sampling. Each old scene normally had one request, allowing image/route shortcuts. This is a plausible cause, not proof that every live miss is caused by task-code neglect. Different teacher scenes show much larger action differences, but those differ in both scene and request and are **not** a matched causal control.

## Bounded transport repair: complete-action task-gated heads

One shared frozen temporal/image backbone was kept. Ten request-indexed final linear maps each predict **four complete 9D pose targets** (36 numeric outputs), not a correction to an existing command. Only those maps were trained on 50 balanced successful SFP train episodes (five for each request); selection used ten complete development episodes. The update cap was 2,000; the nonimprovement rule stopped at 1,000 and chose update 250.

Held-out first-target translation p95 changed **3.236 → 3.013 mm**, a **6.9%** reduction, below the declared 20% threshold. Card 3/port 0 worsened from 2.11 to 2.46 mm; card 4/port 1 remained about 3.79 mm. The request-swap output diameter remained tiny (median **0.736 mm**, nearest alternate **0.216 mm**). This head is **not promoted**, and no live rollout used it. Offline teacher-state imitation error is not safe autonomous arrival.

## Coarse identity on actual autonomous views

The three saved actor runs supplied **111 synchronized three-camera RGB/state rows**, one per simulated second from 4–40 s. The join checks the same trial and a ≤75 ms camera/row gap; simulator port positions were added **after** sampling for scoring only. Candidate selection and crops never used those true positions. The requested opening projects inside all three full images on 8/37, 36/37, and 3/37 rows respectively. Projection inside a frame does **not** prove that the opening is visually identifiable.

The frozen full-image row detector, followed by an observation-only direct-or-rail crop proposal, proposed 27/111 crops; the true selected opening lay inside all three 480 px crops in 17/27. On the card 4/port 1 actor run, 0/9 proposals contained the requested opening. The frozen best crop head accepted **5/27**, made **zero wrong accepts**, and acquired an anchor in only **1/3** actor episodes. All five accepted rows were from card 0/port 0. This is a safety/coverage failure on reused development routes, not a certified visibility test. Native review crops with **posthoc true-position markers** are in `review_crops/`; those markers are diagnostic labels and were never model inputs or independent human visibility labels.

## Next discriminating experiment and budget

Six **train-only same-board, same-robot-reset** paired requests were prepared in [`paired_request_plan_v2/plan.json`](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-05_transport_identity_audit/paired_request_plan_v2/plan.json). Each original request had a previously scored tier-3=75 teacher insertion. The alternate changed only the public selected card and port, covering the other six SFP request codes. The six original requests cover the remaining four codes. This tests the one-request-per-scene shortcut with direct matched action labels. The plan checks source scores and scene/robot hashes; no recorded agent action arrays are used.

Run sequentially on one GPU using [`run_sfp_paired_request_pilot.py`](../../scripts/run_sfp_paired_request_pilot.py). Stop after the first three unless at least two fully insert; require at least four of six full insertions and measured safe buffered arrival before fitting any transport revision. The subsequent matched fit must improve held-out first-target p95 at least 20%, change commands materially under same-image request swaps, and reach the safe buffered region on at least 2/3 **new nonfinal** development starts without more contact. A later promotion gate remains ≥9/10 new safe arrivals. These are stopping rules, not achieved results. Budget: ≤6 sequential teacher episodes (roughly ≤35 minutes of configured 350 s timeouts), one bounded ≤2,000-update fit, then three development rollouts if offline gates pass. Keep final scenes sealed.

The quota field showed `83/1024`, and `/proc/sys/kernel/keys/maxkeys` was 1024. After fixing the parser to use the quota field, all six sequential GPU-1 Gazebo alternates ran. The first three achieved **2/3** full insertions, passing the pilot gate; the full set achieved **5/6**, passing the 4/6 teacher-feasibility gate. Original and alternate scored streams were extracted; five successful alternates supplied 1,290 transport chunks, and six original requests supplied 1,552. The five paired scene/reset groups are disjoint from the existing ten grouped development scenes. The active Isaac GPU-0 process was untouched. The old no-launch statement in the first report is superseded by this correction.

The same-board teachers began from nearly identical TCP positions and diverged by >1 mm after 0.3–0.55 s. Their target actions differed by up to 1.4–8.8 mm **before** that measured separation, depending on the pair. Thus a request-dependent branch is genuinely needed early. After the paths diverge, comparing actions at the same elapsed time also compares different robot states, so it cannot by itself quantify how much code sensitivity the autonomous actor needs. [`paired_teacher_early_divergence_v1.json`](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-05_transport_identity_audit/paired_teacher_early_divergence_v1.json) and [`paired_teacher_action_contrast_v1.json`](../../outputs/experiments/2026-10-05_transport_identity_audit/paired_teacher_action_contrast_v1.json) preserve that distinction. The existing evidence supports a task-conditioning/route-representation hypothesis, but does not prove that weak code use alone caused every missed arrival.

**Decision:** The data-feasibility branch passed, the matched actor branch did not. Do not add routine examples to this failed last-layer recipe. The next targeted design should condition the temporal route decision itself on the requested port, using these same-board pairs to supervise *when and how the route branches*, while retaining the same ten grouped development episodes and an independent new-start live gate. A candidate should be compared to the frozen actor at the first 0.3–1 s branch and on full autonomous safe arrival. No final scene, refiner handoff, insertion policy, or RL promotion follows from the teacher success.
