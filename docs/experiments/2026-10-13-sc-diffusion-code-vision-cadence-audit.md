## What the preceding live comparison established

All matched SC runs were bounded 70-second transport diagnostics, not complete insertion evaluations on every teacher-duration scene. The old 50 ms 12-predict/6-execute actor reached a median closest TCP-to-opening distance of 190 mm on six development starts. The 100 ms 12/6 actor reached 162 mm, and denser 70-second history improved this to 135 mm. These are **relative route improvements**, but all reached 0/6 safe approach bands and 0/6 full insertions. More requested history samples (27 to 38, still over 70 seconds) worsened the six-scene median to 141 mm. Cleaned training rows have a median of only 14 distinct historical frames late in the route; 38 requests therefore did not supply 38 observations.

Deliberate fine-history fitting reached 0.49 mm p95 translation error on recorded **training** actions after three warm-start stages and a train plateau. It still missed 0/2 approach bands on training-scene rollouts at its first stage, 0/6 on development starts at its second stage, and 0/3 after its final plateau. The final three-scene median closest distance was 145 mm. This separates teacher-frame imitation from closed-loop route control. [Preceding matched experiment](2026-10-13-sc-history-drift-followup.md) has checkpoints, training stops and videos.

## Code audit: what is correct, what is weak, and what was fixed

1. **Action coordinates and sampler:** `scripts/build_extended_route_chunks.py` converts recorded SC teacher target poses into the anchor TCP frame. Its manifest reports essentially zero first-four-slot translation reconstruction error. Runtime `RunSharedPortTCP._target_anchor` stores the measured decision-time TCP pose; `_body_delta` converts each predicted relative target into a correction from the current measured TCP. The diffusion trainer's v-prediction and deterministic DDIM-style update use consistent algebra. Saved live RGB/state replay of the fine-history checkpoint on `llb_0090` matched ten recorded first actions within **0.065 mm max** (median 0.022 mm). This makes a gross image preprocessing, task-code or action-frame mismatch unlikely for those decisions. [Exact saved replay](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-13_sc_diffusion_code_audit/fine70_llb0090_input_parity.json>).
2. **Training versus live decision cadence:** The selected teacher cache puts decisions about **0.6 simulated seconds** apart and supervises six 100 ms commands per decision. On matched live SC traces, median proposal gaps were **0.85–0.90 s** (0.80 s with six denoising steps). Inference and post-chunk observation time create roughly 0.20–0.30 s with no newly planned command after each chunk. That changes the observed history, previous-action age, and route progress. This is a measured mismatch and a plausible contributor to early drift, not proof of the sole cause. The actor already issues all six chunk members; this is the interval *between* chunks.
3. **Auxiliary progress label bug for a new cadence:** `route_targets()` previously selected the first saved sample at or after exactly one second. In a 0.9 s cache, that skips the 0.9 s sample and jumps to 1.8 s, outside its 0.8–1.25 s acceptance window. An initial diagnostic fit therefore had **zero valid progress labels** and is invalid as a test of the intended objective. I changed the lookup to choose the nearest future sample within 0.8–1.25 s and added a minimum-label-coverage guard. The existing 0.6 s experiments retained 251 valid route labels among their 252 selected SC validation rows, so this bug does **not** explain their failure. Preserve the failed 0.9 s branch at `.../cadence900_dense70_fit_v1/`; compare only the corrected rerun for model selection.
   The 100 ms derivative also recomputed previous-command tokens without preserving the existing >4 s stale-gap reset. I restored that reset. This does not affect the current SC 100 ms checkpoints, which set prior-command dropout to 1 and pass zeros at deployment, but prevents future nonzero-prior fits from learning across a long data gap.
4. **Fixed sampling noise:** Runtime uses the same seed for each decision. On 24 held-out SC observations and 16 seeds, first-action translation differed from the per-state mean by **0.81 mm median, 1.70 mm p95**. Changing the requested SC port on the same 24 late observations changed the first action by **0.87 mm median**. The diffusion head currently behaves almost deterministically at a given state. This is not a sampler implementation failure, but random seed selection is unlikely to provide substantial left/right route alternatives by itself. [Seed spread](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-13_sc_diffusion_code_audit/dense70_seed_spread.json>) · [port-code swap](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-13_sc_diffusion_code_audit/dense70_port_swap.json>).
5. **Model and supervision limits:** The actor sees three frozen, globally pooled ImageNet ResNet18 vectors, measured 32D state, 10D task code, motion and historical timing. It does not receive card/port pixel locations or spatial feature maps. The 1 s route goal is trained from future measured teacher motion, then predicted by the network during rollout; true future motion is never a runtime input. There is no explicit route mode or progress state, and teacher-only BC does not train recovery from actor-visited states.

## Frozen DINOv2-S representation check

The local official-pretrained DINOv2-S weights and hub implementation were used, with the same selected SC image triplets as the existing ResNet feature cache. The probe sampled 10 valid route rows from each of **83 training and 21 held-out validation episodes** (830/210 rows). For each candidate, a ridge model used measured state and task plus either three ResNet global vectors, three DINO global vectors, or a 4×4 grid of DINO patch vectors. Ridge strength was selected on separate training episodes, then refitted on all training episodes; the 21 validation episodes were not used for choosing strength.

| Feature input | Held-out 1 s TCP-body route error, median / p95 |
| --- | ---: |
| State + task only | **14.78 / 24.37 mm** |
| State + task + pooled ResNet18 | 14.12 / 31.92 mm |
| State + task + pooled DINOv2-S | 14.86 / 35.27 mm |
| State + task + DINOv2-S 4×4 patches | 13.88 / 31.50 mm |

[Exact probe and alpha sweeps](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-13_sc_diffusion_code_audit/dinov2_vs_resnet_route_probe_spatial.json>). This is a **single-frame linear route probe**, not a trained diffusion policy or proof that vision is unnecessary. A nonlinear task-conditioned temporal head might use patch tokens differently. It does show no ready-made advantage from swapping the current pooled ResNet vector for a pooled DINO vector. Existing task-conditioned DINO port-localizer diagnostics also did not establish reliable selected-port identity on native SFP views; see [perception record](2026-10-02-two-family-perception-local-bc.md).

The local DINOv2-S weights contain **22.06M parameters**, versus **11.18M** in the frozen ResNet18 and **14.29M** in the route head. A direct replacement would make this actor approximately **36.35M rather than 25.47M parameters**. On three 224 px views, frozen encoder-only p95 was about **6.66 ms DINO** versus **3.43 ms ResNet** in separate warm, synchronized measurements. These exclude image preprocessing and all diffusion steps. The live ResNet actor already exceeded the 300 ms full-inference goal in its 12-step runs, so a backbone swap cannot be promoted without full-path measurement. The [official DINOv2 model card](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dinov2/blob/main/MODEL_CARD.md) describes the ViT-S/14 and patch outputs; this task-specific result comes from our local probe.

The existing diffusion actor is nevertheless sensitive to its frozen ResNet features: shuffling the visual vectors across scenes on 48 held-out rows moved its first action by **9.76 mm median** (early 11.95 mm, middle 7.55 mm, late 7.81 mm). This out-of-distribution shuffle does **not** show that the visual response is *correct*; it prevents interpreting the state-only linear-probe win as proof that the actor ignores vision. [Sensitivity artifact](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-13_sc_diffusion_code_audit/dense70_visual_sensitivity.json>).

## Corrected cadence test and live result

The immutable derivative `.../route_cache_100ms_900ms_v1/` takes every third 300 ms source-cache row, giving a nominal **0.9 s decision interval** while keeping the recorded 100 ms action slots, the same 83/21 grouped SC episode split, and the same image/action provenance. It has 1,837 valid 12-slot training rows and 697 valid validation rows. The corrected label function produces future progress labels on the selected rows. The fit started from the frozen dense70 checkpoint and changed the decision cadence, not the action frame, horizon, or encoder. The label-free initial run is an invalid control, not evidence about the approach.

The corrected fit reached a validation plateau after **14,500 updates**, selecting update **6,500**. On the **same 252 held-out teacher rows** sampled at 0.9 s, its executed-chunk translation p95 was **6.24 mm**, versus **6.37 mm** for the old 0.6 s model. The nearest-future progress-head p95 was 6.38 versus 19.94 mm, but the two heads were trained against different future intervals, so this is a narrow diagnostic rather than a policy win. ## Decision rule

**The cadence branch failed its 2/3 arrival gate.** Prioritize **safe teacher continuations from actor-visited states** around the first 6–9 s drift, keeping original teacher routes and using actor prefix observations only as context. A bounded feasibility gate is three training-reset takeovers with the current SC LLB planner and real Gazebo cable: at least two must complete a no-contact, no-over-card route to the safe approach region; otherwise stop collection and repair planner initialization or choose explicit route control. If feasible, collect a small varied training set across card counts, selected ports and starts; compare a corrected-data actor with a same-budget control on new grouped development resets. Continue only if early measured route error falls and at least two of three new autonomous starts reach the safe approach band without contact and with p95 inference below 300 ms. If that also fails, test an explicit observed route-progress/short-waypoint head before scaling another diffusion or DINO backbone. Do not speed-edit or regenerate the full dataset until the route gate passes. Keep handoff, insertion, RL and final scenes gated.
