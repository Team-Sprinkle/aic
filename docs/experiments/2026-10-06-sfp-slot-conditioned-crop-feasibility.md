# SFP selected-slot crop feasibility (October 6)

The October 5 slot-local model predicts occupied rails well, but cross-camera rail endpoints are not precise enough for a strict 3D board-pose verifier. A two-view fit got references in 10/10 nonfinal teacher routes but only 2/10 early correct port candidates; constraining the rail to a horizontal 160 mm CAD line fell to 4/10 references and 1/10 early correct. Those failures are preserved in [the robust rail check](2026-10-05-sfp-robust-two-view-rail-check.md). This experiment tests a different use of the learned slot layout: center a **native RGB crop** on the *requested rail* using its model-predicted 2D endpoints, then train a local request-conditioned port detector only if the crop includes the requested opening often enough.

The frozen slot-local checkpoint supplies every crop center. The task code supplies the requested card and port. True port projections are used **only after crop construction to score coverage**, never to choose a crop. Use the unchanged grouped ten-scene development and ten further disjoint nonfinal teacher-route sets; report the three saved sparse actor routes separately if suitable labels are available. Fixed crop sizes 160, 320 and **480 px** are inspected, with 480 px the existing broad-refiner size and the **predeclared training candidate**. The feasibility gate for a 480 px crop is a selected opening inside that crop in at least two cameras within the first three saved seconds on **≥9/10 groups in both ten-scene sets**. Also report when other cards' ports share the crop, and whether the selected occupied-slot vote is valid. Projected-in-frame is not true visual visibility; this is necessary coverage only.

If the 480 px crop fails, do not train a local detector around it. If it passes, fit one bounded request-conditioned head from original RGB crops and the selected-port task code on **train reset groups only**, using held-out complete reset groups for checkpoint choice and a separate disjoint stress set for final diagnostic. A model must reject wrong-card/absent requests and preserve useful early coverage; otherwise it is not a handoff solution. No true port pose or simulator geometry may enter the actor, crop selector or autonomous evaluation. Automatic switching, insertion and RL remain disabled. Final scenes stay sealed.

## Crop coverage and image review

The frozen crop centers passed the **480 px necessary-coverage gate**: within the first three seconds, the selected opening projected inside the crop in at least two cameras on **10/10 checkpoint-development** and **10/10 extra disjoint nonfinal** groups; all three views qualified on 10/10 in both sets. A 320 px crop reached 9/10 and 10/10 in two views; a 160 px crop reached only 2/10 and 2/10. These are projections, not certified visual visibility.

The 480 px crop also contained a port on **another card** in at least two views on 9/10 development and 10/10 extra groups. [Original RGB sample crops with target/other projections](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_slot_crop_visual_check>) confirm that the selected pair and neighboring card openings can appear together. The next model must use the centered rail and requested port code to resolve that ambiguity; mere crop containment is insufficient.

The bounded fit will use the existing three-camera 480→320 request-conditioned ResNet18 crop architecture with a hard-neighbor loss, initialized from the earlier crop checkpoint, and train on the 70 train reset groups only. Frozen slot-local rail predictions supply every crop center, including for train rows. Physically present alternative card/port requests from each train frame supply counterfactual supervision; the ten checkpoint-development groups retain their recorded request for checkpoint selection. Budget: one GPU, at most **2,000 updates or 120 minutes**, with complete reset groups kept separate. Select by the existing early-safety score. On the extra ten disjoint nonfinal groups, require **zero accepted wrong ports**, at least **9/10 early correct groups**, and accepted selected-port 3D p95 ≤20 mm before a new autonomous test. A failure stops this crop architecture; no threshold tuning on the extra scenes. The strict final-scene split remains sealed.

## Completed bounded fit and decision, October 6

The frozen slot-local head supplied crop centers for **3,690 training frames / 70 reset groups**. These were expanded to **21,262 physically present requested-card/port examples**; checkpoint choice used the separate **119-frame / ten-group** development set. The crop model finished its full **2,000-update** cap on GPU 1 and selected update **1,800**. Development: **38/119 accepted**, **zero accepted wrong**, **2.15 mm accepted 3D p95**, but only **6/10 early groups**. This missed the early gate.

We still scored the selected checkpoint once on the fixed **581-frame / ten-group additional nonfinal** set to understand generalization, comparing the old checkpoint on precisely the same predicted-rail crops. The old checkpoint accepted **38/581**, including **one wrong port**, at **4.46 mm** accepted 3D p95 and **6/10 early groups**. The new checkpoint accepted **62/581**, **zero wrong**, at **1.80 mm** accepted 3D p95, but remained **6/10 early groups**. Its accepted estimates are accurate when three cameras agree; sparse acceptance is the failure. These are projected CAD reference errors after the fact, not runtime geometry. The overall handoff gate **failed**, so no model was promoted to autonomous transport, insertion or RL.

The four missing early groups were card1/port0, card1/port1, card2/port0 and card3/port1. In the first three seconds, all three projected openings lay inside the crops in 7/7, 5/7, 7/7 and 7/7 frames, respectively. Three cameras simultaneously passed the model's 0.5 probability threshold in 5/7, 7/7, 7/7 and 7/7 frames. Yet **all of those confident triples failed the 5 px reprojection consistency check**. In an original-RGB overlay for card1/port0, one camera chooses the neighboring opening/card despite a high flag probability. Thus the remaining gap is cross-view **port identity correspondence**, not crop coverage or a simple lack of model confidence. Green circles are independent model predictions and red circles are posthoc simulator projections in [four failure panels](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_slot_crop_failure_views_v1/>). The labels were not used to place crops or make predictions.
