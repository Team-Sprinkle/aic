## Checkpoint provenance correction and data decision

A subsequent exact manifest/code audit changes the interpretation of the v9 failure. `scripts/run_clean_transport_bc.sh` always passes `--warm-start /var/tmp/chmin_aic_20260918_act/shared_body_target_bc_batch3_20260925/best.pt`; `scripts/train_clean_transport_bc.py` loads **all matching model weights, including the complete-target action head**, excluding only the obsolete elapsed-time module. It freezes the old pose head, while the visual/context/action modules are trainable. The source checkpoint was at update 3,750 and lists old canonical, supplemental, diversity, five-card-lift and teacher-correction SC sources alongside SFP. These sources predate the current route admission and are a **mixture**, not a certified low outside-row route set. The source list does not prove every old SC trajectory crossed over cards, nor quantify how many did. Therefore v9 was **not a clean test of learning the new SC route from uncontaminated initialization**. Two new full-success SC episodes may be too little to overwrite a mixed old route prior; this is a plausible cause, not established causal attribution from the current rollouts.

The actual v9 training sample was **236 SFP-to-NIC episodes (132,303 rows)** plus **two SC-to-SC full-success episodes (1,751 transport rows)**. One SC episode selected port 0, one port 1; both used the exact same five-card physical world. Validation included 32 episode-grouped SFP episodes (1,024 sampled rows for periodic offline checks) and two separate SC route-only episodes (512 sampled rows); one SC validation teacher itself missed the endpoint and is diagnostic only. Balanced sampling gave SFP and SC equal aggregate probability, then split SC equally by selected port. The 1,751 SC rows are two correlated demonstrations, not 1,751 independent cases.

The 268 verified SFP source episodes are also **not exclusively original CheatCode**. Their provenance paths show 130 accepted mixed-card CheatCode episodes and ten one-card CheatCode smoke episodes; the remaining 128 come from later nominal, corrective, student and multicard sources. All were in the verified source, but the next dataset must explicitly separate original CheatCode success from later correction sources and admit each by scored success and causal command contract. No autonomous SFP-to-NIC rollout was run for this v9 model, so its SFP closed-loop performance and regression are unknown; only SFP offline rows were checked.

Name the desired SC route **Low Lateral Bypass (LLB)**: move back, move left while descending below the card-top clearance, move forward beside the row, move right, tilt, move back, then descend to the opening. The directions describe the current scene coordinates, not a universal task-frame controller. The actual planned route and below-card clearance matter more than the name. For future BC, build a provenance-controlled set with verified SFP-to-NIC CheatCode successes and verified SC-to-SC LLB successes, varied across selected ports, card layouts and start/reset configurations. Exclude failed insertion actions; retain valid transport-only prefixes separately if a successful full episode is unavailable. Hold out whole physical resets/layouts. A clean model should initialize its vision backbone from ImageNet and its policy/action modules without the old mixed-SC checkpoint, or use a documented SFP-only checkpoint; compare it against the old warm start under the same admitted data and evaluation. Do not claim the current two SC episodes provide enough route diversity. This corrects the priority of the previously proposed route-subgoal experiment: first resolve initialization and route-data provenance, then diagnose whether the clean supervised actor still needs route-phase/subgoal structure. No new training was started for this audit.
