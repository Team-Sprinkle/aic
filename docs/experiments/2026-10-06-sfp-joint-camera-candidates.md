# SFP requested-port joint camera candidates

## Frozen design before development scoring

The [preceding crop fit](2026-10-06-sfp-slot-conditioned-crop-feasibility.md) gave accurate accepted poses but only 6/10 early scene groups. On failure frames, the selected opening is usually in the native-image crop and all three camera heads are confident, yet one head chooses a neighboring opening. This experiment keeps that checkpoint and all scene splits frozen. It tests a **different inference rule**, not another training run.

For each of the three original-image 480 px crops, retain the top **five** local maxima of the request-conditioned heatmap, with **9 heatmap-cell** nonmaximum suppression. For every three-view combination, triangulate using observed TCP and fixed camera calibration. Rank by maximum reprojection residual in pixels plus **2 × the sum of the per-view heatmap-logit gaps** from each view's best peak. Accept only when all three view flags are at least 0.5 and the winning triple's reprojection residual is at most **5 px**. These constants are fixed before scoring; simulator target pose or identity is used only after prediction to grade the result. Do not tune the constants on the ten already-scored additional scenes.

First run this rule on the same ten complete development groups. A useful development signal is zero accepted wrong ports and at least 9/10 groups with an accepted pose within three seconds of the first saved proposal, with accepted 3D p95 at most 20 mm. If it passes, evaluate once on **new** nonfinal reset groups and measure full inference latency; if not, diagnose from saved heatmaps and stop this rule. The already-scored ten additional teacher-route groups remain an inspected stress set and will not be reused for selecting this rule. No autonomous handoff, insertion or RL follows solely from teacher-route replay. Final scenes remain sealed.

## First frozen rule and one bounded persistence repair

The fixed top-five rule produced 72 accepted development frames and early coverage in **10/10** groups, but accepted **two wrong-card points**: card1/port0 at 3.85 s chose card2/port0 (53.4 mm error), and card3/port0 at 3.85 s chose card2/port0 (45.5 mm error). Both were the **first accepted frame** of their group, with apparently good 1.67 and 3.39 px reprojection. Thus cross-view geometric agreement alone can be consistent on the wrong card. [Predictions and summary](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_joint_crop_development_v1/>).

One bounded repair is fixed before running it: accept a joint candidate only after **two consecutive** saved proposals in the same episode both pass the original three-view check and their predicted fixed-port positions differ by at most **10 mm**. The port itself is static; the second camera inspection acts as causal confirmation. No true port pose participates in this decision. This may delay or miss handoff, so keep the original zero-wrong, ≥9/10 early, ≤20 mm accepted p95 development gate. Do not tune the 10 mm or two-frame constants on the additional ten already-scored groups.

## Development result and fresh ten-scene decision

The fixed two-inspection rule passed on the ten checkpoint-development groups: **59 accepted frames, zero wrong, 10/10 early groups, 3.27 mm accepted 3D p95**. This was only a development signal, since its design followed inspection of the first rule's two wrong frames there. I then froze ten further complete **nonfinal source-development resets**, one per requested card/port code. Their hashes have no overlap with the 70 train groups, ten checkpoint-development groups or previously scored additional ten groups. All ten ordinary CheatCode camera controls fully inserted (official tier-3 = 75). They supplied **528** synchronized 0.5 s image triplets, **527** retained after the board-label synchronization filter. The frozen slot-local board model got exact occupancy maps on 10/10 of these full-card scenes. The new teacher insertions validate the camera capture; they are **not autonomous insertions by our policy**.

On these 527 observations, the **unfiltered joint top-five rule** accepted 171 frames but made **12 wrong-port acceptances**, accepted 3D p95 **42.44 mm**, and had 10/10 early groups. The **frozen two-inspection gate** retained 150 accepts with **eight wrong-port acceptances** and **25.21 mm** accepted p95, still 10/10 early. It **failed** both the zero-wrong and ≤20 mm accuracy gates. No constants were changed after opening these scenes.

For a matched control, the existing frozen **single-point crop model** on the exact same images and predicted-rail crops accepted **89/527** with **zero wrong ports**, **2.71 mm** accepted p95 and **10/10 early groups**. This control performed better than the joint method here, but it had reached only **6/10 early** on the previously scored additional ten-scene set; therefore it still does not establish a robust automatic handoff. Do not promote either path.

Posthoc error attribution found two persistent neighbor-card mistakes: card1/port0 was called card2/port0 on two accepted frames, around **53 mm** away; card3/port1 was called card4/port1 on six accepted frames, around **42 mm** away. Their three-camera reprojection residuals remained below 5 px, and the latter mistake persisted for six successive half-second frames. The [card1 panel](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_joint_candidate_fresh10_failure_overlays_v1/card1_port0.jpg>) and [card3 panel](</home/nas/chmin/aic_archives/2026-10-06_sfp_joint_candidate_fresh10_failure_overlays_v1/card3_port1.jpg>) were inspected on original RGB: green is the selected joint candidate, red is the requested opening's posthoc projection, and magenta is the neighboring opening's posthoc projection. Both wrong candidates visibly point to another card. Three-camera reprojection and temporal persistence cannot by themselves establish **which numbered card** was requested.

**Decision and next hypothesis:** Stop the unstructured top-five fusion branch. The safer single-point checkpoint still fails consistency across the two ten-scene checks (6/10 versus 10/10 early), while the transport actor remains 0/3 at safe buffered arrival. Next build **explicit card identity** into the visual representation: predict the requested card's board slot and its two port candidates together, with a grouped, task-balanced data audit and reviewed ambiguous RGB examples. Treat a card-ID failure as abstention. Evaluate on new grouped nonfinal starts; only then reconsider automatic handoff. Do not collect more routine teacher routes or tune thresholds on these fresh ten scenes. Full live latency is deferred because the correctness gate failed; the frozen board component's 83.8 ms p95 does not cover crop refiner, ROS or actor.
