# SFP actor-state restore feasibility, bounded preflight

## Question and frozen gate

Can one nonfinal Gazebo actor takeover be restored twice to the *same complete dynamical state* before comparing candidate routes? The predeclared gate in [the stage-label audit](2026-10-10-sfp-stage-aware-transport-label-gate.md#decision-and-next-repair) required TCP position ≤2 mm, orientation ≤1°, proximal cable-link position RMS ≤5 mm, first-second wrist-force p95 difference ≤2 N, and restored controller target and joint velocities. Two no-action restores and a same-action replay were conditional on a usable complete-state restore mechanism. Budget: one GPU and at most two hours. Final scenes stayed sealed.

## What I checked

1. Read the local Gazebo world and AIC reset code. The task uses Gazebo Sim 9.5.0 with `bullet-featherstone` physics. `ResetJointsPlugin` only sets named joint positions and zero velocities; it does not capture the actor takeover, cable geometry or controller command state. The engine resets trials by spawning/deleting entities, not by restoring a saved takeover.
2. Started a disposable rootless, headless **minimal** Gazebo world using the same installed Gazebo version and Bullet Featherstone physics, with `UserCommands`. This was a service-interface probe, not an AIC episode or a restore trial. Its exact SDF and service list are at `outputs/experiments/2026-10-10_sfp_state_restore_feasibility_v1/`. The image was `ghcr.io/intrinsic-dev/aic/aic_eval@sha256:9aa2ffdbb946d38edde1bac7b5f02a44cfbea26e3b04a9c74e09f14c97472923`; no GPU was allocated. Only the unrelated preexisting Isaac container remained running afterward.
3. The probe exposes `/world/restore_probe/state` (`Empty → SerializedStepMap`) and `/world/restore_probe/control/state` (`WorldControlState → Boolean`). The latter can supply serialized **entity-component** state. There are also pose-setting and log-playback services. These interfaces alone do not capture all state of the live AIC stack.
4. Read `aic_controller/src/aic_controller.cpp`. The controller's current target, previous tool/joint references, remaining interpolation time, target mode and motion update are private in-process variables, updated on each control cycle. The Gazebo ECS state service does not serialize that ROS controller process. Re-sending a hold or deactivating the controller changes the state at the branch point; neither is an exact restore of the saved target and interpolation phase.
5. Read `aic_gazebo/src/CablePlugin.cc`. The cable attachment state machine and timing fields are private plugin variables; its `Reset` method only logs a message. The Gazebo ECS service does not demonstrate restoration of these fields or of Bullet Featherstone's internal contacts/solver state. The [Gazebo ECM documentation](https://gazebosim.org/api/sim/9/EntityComponentManager_8hh_source.html) describes entity/component state, and the [physics documentation](https://gazebosim.org/api/sim/9/physics.html) identifies Bullet Featherstone as a separate physics engine. Neither establishes a complete AIC branch snapshot.

## Commands and result

The minimal-world probe used the repository's rootless Docker socket and `gz sim -s -r /probe/probe_world.sdf`; then `gz service -l` and `gz service -i` queried the above services. The service output was saved to `outputs/experiments/2026-10-10_sfp_state_restore_feasibility_v1/service_probe.txt`. The container `aic_restore_service_probe_20261010` was stopped and removed. No AIC actor, Gazebo episode, model fit, final evaluation or GPU job was started by this check.

**Outcome: failed preflight / no fidelity measurements.** The prerequisite complete-state capture-and-restore path is absent. Therefore the proposed two no-action AIC restores and same-action replay were **not run**: their numerical tolerances remain unmeasured, and no route-ranking data is admitted. A successful ECS/pose service response would not satisfy the controller-state gate. The five older same-config starts already differ by 11.83–31.07 mm at actor takeover and cannot substitute for matched states.

## Decision

Close the exact-state route-ranking branch under the current tooling. Building a genuine snapshot would require explicit controller export/import, cable-plugin state capture, joint velocities, and a physics/contact-state fidelity test; this is a distinct engineering project, not a hidden addition to this bounded experiment. The next bounded transport experiment should use **on-policy, state-conditioned feedback**: preserve whole-reset groups, capture each actor's actual three-camera history, robot state, commanded route, force, measured cable contact and outcome, and test whether a cable-risk predictor improves route decisions over a robot-state/route-only baseline. The overhead camera is diagnostic only.

**Proposed next gate, to freeze before collection:** first inventory existing contact-instrumented actor prefixes and count independent reset groups and positive cable/NIC-card contacts. If there are fewer than six positive and six negative development groups, do not fit a classifier from the current archive. A targeted prospective diagnostic may then use at most 30 train and ten new development reset groups, one GPU, four hours of Gazebo time, with varied requested ports and card count and no final scenes. Label a future 1 s contact risk at the observed decision (peak cable/NIC-card point force >10 N), and report group-held-out precision/recall plus false alarms separately for camera-history+proprio+force and proprio+route+force baselines. Require ≥90% recall with ≤20% false-alarm rate and a material improvement over the baseline before trying at most three nonfinal autonomous feedback starts. Promotion still requires ≥2/3 safe buffered arrivals, no >10 N cable/NIC-card point-force event, no worse official score, and p95 inference <300 ms; otherwise stop this branch and revisit observation/route design. The contact oracle supplies training/evaluation labels only, never policy input. These numbers are a **proposed next experiment**, not results or a retroactive gate. No new actor or route ranker fit follows from the current evidence. Automatic handoff, insertion, RL and final scenes remain closed.
