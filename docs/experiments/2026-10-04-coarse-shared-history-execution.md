# Shared coarse crop and stop model: execution plan

**Status:** bounded coarse diagnostic complete, October 5, 2026; safe handoff gate failed and remains open. **Final IK scenes:** sealed. **GPU ceiling:** four concurrently; preserve active Isaac process PID 468750.

## Updated full pipeline

1. **Transport actor** uses public task code, RGB, state and motion history to approach the requested port. SFP arrival is currently the main failure, with 0/3 safe buffered visits in a frozen autonomous comparison; SC has limited shadow successes. A better coarse model cannot rescue a route that never presents identifiable target evidence.
2. **Shared coarse history model** runs during approach on current three-camera RGB plus measured-distance history. The requested-port crop head proposes regions in the **current original-resolution** images. A proposal-conditioned identity/approach head decides continue versus pause-and-inspect. The old late camera-check function belongs here. It may abstain. No true simulator geometry enters its inference.
3. **Precise selected-port refiner** runs on accepted current crops and estimates the one requested port's pose. Its result and current RGB/state feed the port-relative insertion actor only after identity, pose, orientation, force and stable-hold checks. The coarse decision alone never starts insertion.
4. **Insertion actor** predicts complete targets in its estimated selected-port frame; controller conversion uses measured TCP. Hardcoded local/hierarchical backtracking remains a separate controlled recovery experiment. Offline/online SERL and Gazebo adaptation stay gated on autonomous supervised insertion and safe failure behavior.
5. **Transport repair remains parallel but distinct:** audit early route drift, teacher-versus-own previous-command mismatch, data balance, and spatial route context. Do not interpret coarse image prediction as a transport success.

3. Reproduce frozen baselines and build 30 mm measured-path history (up to twelve three-camera anchors), resetting by episode. Preserve current frame at every decision; older frames supply context. Compare current-only and history models with identical splits, image encoder and update budgets. Use a shared spatial backbone; crop decoder works on current features. 4. Freeze a threshold on development episodes. Gate: zero accepted wrong-port proposals, ≥9/10 identifiable early SFP requests where observable, ≥90% accepted crop coverage with 20 px margin, no false unsafe stop in existing wrong-route/SC negatives, and complete p95 inference <300 ms. 5. If failed, inspect raw wrong/missed examples and pose/identity traces. 6. Only after a fresh held-out pass, connect accepted coarse crops to the precise refiner in a shadow run, then test actual transport-to-insertion handoff. Preserve failures, commands, checkpoints, predictions, overlays and latency. Do not open reserved final scenes or activate RL from offline crop accuracy alone.

**Upstream geometry finding:** The broad-crop constructor assumes a 40 mm row shift perpendicular to the observed two-port vector. Across 181 adjacent-card pairs in source-training scene groups, the actual base-frame row delta had median `(1.5, 39.9)` mm but x ranged approximately **−19.1 to +24.1 mm** between the 10th and 90th percentiles. The card positions vary independently along that axis. Thus a single source card and nominal shift can miss the requested card by several centimeters even when the source pair is measured accurately. A robust two-coefficient affine calibration changed grouped-development nonzero-gap proposal error only from 39.28/60.18 to 35.14/53.98 mm median/p90. The stronger next representation must locate the **individual requested card** using native image evidence and full-row context; more crop-only losses, frozen low-resolution history, or a fixed spacing correction cannot supply its random lateral offset.

### Native full-row locator: bounded next representation

The [new trainer](../../scripts/train_sfp_native_row_locator.py) fine-tunes the existing 12-channel ResNet-18 detector on the same 66 grouped training episodes. It reads all three original 1152×1024 cameras at 576×512 for its single-camera shared backbone, double the earlier 288×256 width and height. Each present port has its own spatial heatmap; the public request selects the required channel. The loss emphasizes the requested port while retaining supervision on other ports in the same image, so per-card lateral variation can be learned from visual detail rather than imputed by fixed spacing. Its grouped ten-episode development split, 1,800-update cap, 45-minute wall cap, and 4e−5 learning rate are fixed. The [native candidate audit](../../scripts/audit_sfp_card_row_association.py) accepts an explicit input resolution and will score checkpoint-selected port hypotheses in three-view base geometry. This is a distinct representation experiment, not another fit of the same broad-crop assumption. It still cannot certify actual visibility or safe stopping until independently reviewed labels exist. ## Decision and revised next experiment

The **zero wrong plus at least nine of ten early** coarse handoff gate remains open. No result is a safe switch; no autonomous insertion, RL, final IK evaluation, or full live latency test was run from these checkpoints. Further generic crop-head fitting on the same 66/10 split is stopped. The next bounded repair must establish **which physical slot** an observed card occupies and **whether its requested opening is actually identifiable**, using independently checked labels. The source-board position, sparse-card gaps and independent x shifts must be represented explicitly. First audit actual autonomous transport pauses and the existing marked/unmarked early images for (a) requested card offscreen, (b) visible but ordinally ambiguous, and (c) visible with correct slot evidence. Then test one shared current-view full-row slot/port representation with distance-sampled history, or a collision-checked active view if the target cannot be seen. Train only on complete training reset groups and fix an independent new-development acquisition and wrong-accept gate before opening more scenes. If the target remains unidentifiable under safe viewpoints, revise transport to obtain a better observation; do not supply true simulator geometry or lower the acceptance bar. The selected-port refiner, insertion actor, and RL remain disconnected until that gate and complete p95 <300 ms pass.
