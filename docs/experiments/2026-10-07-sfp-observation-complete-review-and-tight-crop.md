**Scope:** saved nonfinal SFP images and frozen RGB rail proposals only. No training, new Gazebo run, final-scene access, actor change or RL in this gate.

## Distinct crop hypothesis and stopping rule

The current **480 px** original-image crop around the RGB-predicted selected rail often contains neighboring cards. A fixed **320 px** crop may retain the requested port while excluding those same-port neighbors, giving the existing one-point locator a less ambiguous input. This is a geometry-only feasibility audit, not an image-model score. The selected port and neighboring-card projections are used **only after crop centers are generated by frozen RGB predictions** to count coverage; they never choose a crop at inference.

Use the immutable 70-train/ten-development label/proposal materialization. Freeze size = 320 px before checking. Compare with existing 480 px on the same grouped development rows. A useful crop requires the requested port to be inside at least two camera crops within the first three saved seconds in **≥9/10** development episodes, and in those two cameras **no same-port neighboring-card opening** inside the crop. If that fails, stop the tight-crop branch without training. If it passes, lock the design before a read-only check on the ten newer nonfinal groups, and only then consider one matched training fit. Do not tune size on the newer groups.

The nominal base-frame port prior has already been falsified as a stop/identity cue: development median port error **44.6 mm** and false autonomous stops in saved replay. It may remain a weak scheduling prior, but it is not repeated as a transport fix here. [Prior audit](2026-10-05-global-coordinate-arrival-proxy.md).

## Completed read-only crop gate

The fixed 320 px audit on the same ten complete development groups found the requested opening inside at least two RGB-predicted crops early in **9/10** episodes, but found an early *clean* two-camera crop (requested opening inside, every other same-port card opening outside) in only **6/10**. The existing 480 px crop scored **10/10** selected-in-two-cameras and **3/10** clean. Narrowing the crop helps distinguish neighbors in three more scenes but loses the requested point in card-0/port-0 and still includes a neighbor for card-1/port-0, card-1/port-1 and card-3/port-0. This misses the predeclared ≥9/10 clean gate, so **stop without a 320 px model fit or newer-scene test**. Do not tune crop size on the ten newer groups.
